To rule out the possibility that depth classifiers merely exploit generator identity, we train a binary classifier (generation~1 vs.\ generation~2+) on each of the eight generators and evaluate it on the remaining seven (Table~\ref{tab:transfer}, Figure~\ref{fig:transfer}). The mean cross-generator transfer F1 is 0.810 (95\% bootstrap CI: [0.796,\,0.822]), significantly above chance ($t=46.58$, $p<1.0e-10$). Transfer is strongest from Py-2.8B$\to$Py-1B (F1\,=\,0.87) and weakest from Py-1B$\to$Mi-7B (F1\,=\,0.62), yet even the weakest pair exceeds the 0.5 baseline. These results confirm that generational depth signatures generalize across architectures and are not an artifact of generator identity.
