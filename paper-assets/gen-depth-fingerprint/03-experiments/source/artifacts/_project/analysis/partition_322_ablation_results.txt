--- Key findings ---
1. The 3/2/2 partition yields a substantially higher silhouette score (+0.1561),
   driven primarily by tail_decay's improved fit (+0.9026 per-feature delta).
2. tail_decay has very low |rho| (0.041) and d (0.028), placing it close to
   entropy_std in (|rho|, d) space — both are non-drifting features with
   minimal depth-dependent shift.
3. The k=2 binary partition (drifting vs non-drifting) achieves the highest
   silhouette (0.7352) and is identical under both 3/1/3 and 3/2/2.
4. Classification (LOGO F1) uses all 7 features regardless of partition;
   the ablation tests partition interpretability, not classification performance.

--- Recommendation ---
Report the 3/2/2 alternative as a robustness check. The higher silhouette
supports the reviewer's intuition that tail_decay is a boundary case better
grouped with intermediate features. The main narrative (drifting vs non-drifting
separation) is unaffected (k=2 silhouette = 0.7352 in both cases).
