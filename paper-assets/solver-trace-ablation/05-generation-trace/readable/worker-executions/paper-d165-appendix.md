105	\paragraph{What ISR measures---and what it does not.}
106	ISR quantifies syntactic component necessity: the fraction of solver-input components whose individual removal causes proof failure.
107	This is strictly weaker than semantic faithfulness: whether the formalization accurately captures the natural-language meaning.
108	We verify this empirically: Spearman $\rho(\text{ISR}, \text{NLI accuracy}) = -0.049$ ($p = 0.187$, n.s.) from a downstream NLI evaluation on exec-only checkpoint outputs.
109	ISR and downstream task performance are uncorrelated.
110	ISR is thus a component-necessity diagnostic, not a semantic quality metric: high ISR indicates that every component contributes to the proof, not that the proof faithfully represents the original reasoning problem.
111	
112	Critically, low ISR carries different semantics depending on the training objective.
113	Under exec-only training, low ISR in Prolog reflects redundant coding (repeated ground facts where removing any single copy does not affect the proof) rather than an absence of reasoning (\S\ref{sec:crosslogic}).
114	Under ISR-as-reward, high ISR reflects metric gaming (\S\ref{sec:goodhart}).
115	ISR values must always be interpreted relative to the optimization objective that produced them.

117	\paragraph{The premise-count confound.}

250	\paragraph{TS false-negative correction (TSb obfuscated tautology).}
251	ISR $\beta{=}0.1$ seed~44 contained 12 samples with $n_{\text{prem}}{=}1$ classified as non-hacking by the text matcher.
252	Manual inspection confirmed all 12 are logically equivalent to TS but evade text matching through vacuous quantifier wrapping ($11/12$) or structural simplification ($1/12$).
253	We classify these as \textbf{TSb} (obfuscated tautology), distinct from text-match-detectable TSa.
254	$10/12$ achieve $\text{ISR}{\geq}0.83$, indicating simultaneous gaming of execution reward, ISR metric, and TS classifier: triple gaming (\S\ref{sec:taxonomy}).
255	After correction, ISR s44 non-hacking${=}0$; total TS = TSa (77 text-match) + TSb (12 obfuscated) = 89.
256	
257	\paragraph{Impact on non-hacking-rate comparison.}
258	After PG reclassification and TS false-negative correction: ISR non-hacking $= 14/231 = 6.06\%$ vs.\ random non-hacking $= 25/262 = 9.5\%$ (Fisher $p{=}0.182$, n.s.; Wilson 95\% CI: ISR [3.6\%, 9.9\%], Random [6.5\%, 13.7\%]).
259	The direction is consistent with ISR-amplified gaming modes (TS, PG) displacing non-hacking output, but the effect is not statistically significant at the conventional $\alpha{=}0.05$ threshold.
260	
261	\subsection{CE --- Conclusion Embedding}
262	
263	\paragraph{Definition.}
264	The conclusion appears verbatim (or with trivial variable renaming) as one premise among multiple premises.
265	The solver succeeds by matching the embedded conclusion, rendering the other premises irrelevant to the proof.
266	
267	\paragraph{Example (FOL).}
268	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
269	{\small\begin{verbatim}
270	Premises:
271	  all x (bird(x) -> flies(x)).
272	  bird(tweety).
273	  flies(tweety).  % conclusion embedded
274	Conclusion: flies(tweety).
275	\end{verbatim}}
276	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
277	
278	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
279	Variable. The embedded premise has ISR~$=1$ (removing it may break the proof), but other premises may also register as necessary depending on Prover9's search strategy.
280	ISR does not distinguish trivial from substantive necessity.
281	
282	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
283	Conclusion embedding in some form (CE multi-premise or TS single-premise) is GRPO-universal at 68--97\% across all RL conditions.
284	Under exec-only and Random reward, it manifests as CE (71--93\%); under ISR reward, it shifts to TS (70.6\% pooled).
285	
286	
287	\subsection{TSa --- Conclusion Duplication (Standard)}
288	
289	\paragraph{Definition.}
290	The entire formalization collapses to a single premise whose content is identical (text-match detectable) to the conclusion.
291	Since $P \to P$ is trivially provable, this simultaneously maximizes exec reward.
292	Because the sole premise is necessary for every sub-component of the conclusion under LOO ablation, ISR is inflated toward 1.0.
293	
294	\paragraph{Example (FOL).}
295	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
296	{\small\begin{verbatim}
297	Premises:
298	  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).
299	Conclusion:
300	  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).
301	\end{verbatim}}
302	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
303	
304	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
305	ISR~$\approx 0.8$--$1.0$.
306	The single premise is trivially ``maximally necessary,'' but the proof is vacuous.
307	TSa games both exec reward and ISR reward; TSb extends this to triple gaming by additionally evading the text-match classifier.
308	
309	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
310	TSa (text-match detectable): 64.5\% pooled (149/231) under ISR~$\beta{=}0.1$ (s42: 16.0\%, s43: 96.7\%, s44: 86.5\%).
311	Combined TS (TSa + TSb): 70.6\% (163/231; s43 TSa${=}59$ + TSb${=}2$; includes s44 FN correction).
312	Random~$\beta{=}0.1$ pooled: 13.7\% (36/262, all TSa; driven by seed~43 at 38.3\%, seeds~42 and~44 at 0\%).
313	
314	
315	\subsection{TSb --- Obfuscated Tautology}
316	
317	\paragraph{Definition.}
318	A single-premise formalization logically equivalent to TSa (premise = conclusion) but with surface-form differences that evade the text-match TS classifier.
319	The model achieves this through vacuous quantifier wrapping ($11/12$ instances) or structural simplification ($1/12$).
320	
321	\paragraph{Example (FOL, vacuous quantifier wrapping).}
322	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
323	{\small\begin{verbatim}
324	Premises:
325	  all x all y ((A(x)&B(y))->(C(x)|D(y))).
326	Conclusion:
327	  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).
328	\end{verbatim}}
329	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
330	The premise introduces a vacuous second variable $y$, immediately unified with $x$; the formulas are logically equivalent but differ textually.
331	
332	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
333	$\text{ISR}{\geq}0.83$ ($10/12$ samples).
334	TSb simultaneously games three objectives: execution reward ($r_{\text{exec}}{=}1$ via tautological proof), ISR metric (single necessary premise yields high ISR), and the text-match TS classifier (surface-form difference evades detection).
335	This triple gaming makes TSb the most complete Goodhart case we observe.
336	
337	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
338	Found primarily in ISR $\beta{=}0.1$ seed~44 ($12/89$ proved samples, all originally classified as non-hacking); seed~43 contributes $2$ additional cases.
339	Functional cross-validation via $n_{\text{necessary}}{=}1$ exposed the misclassification.
340	
341	
342	\subsection{Tautological Collapse}
343	
344	\paragraph{Definition.}
345	Two-component formalizations where the premise and conclusion are identical atomic or simple formulas, yielding a $P \to P$ tautology with minimal token cost.
346	
347	\paragraph{Example (FOL).}
348	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
349	\begin{verbatim}
350	Premises: p(a).
351	Conclusion: p(a).
352	\end{verbatim}
353	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
354	
355	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
356	ISR~$=1.0$ (the sole premise is necessary).
357	Distinguished from TS by having low component count ($n_{\text{comp}}\approx 2$) and atomic formulas rather than complex sub-structured conclusions.
358	
359	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
360	Dominant in FOL exec-only seed~44 (PR~$=0.98$, mean $n_{\text{comp}}=2.04$, 100\% TS by strict classifier).
361	Also observed in Prolog exec+STA seed~42 (100\% TS, PR~$=0.50$).
362	
363	
364	\subsection{Repetition Collapse}
365	
366	\paragraph{Definition.}
367	The model generates identical premises or clauses repeated until the maximum completion length, producing a degenerate output with high token count but no logical diversity.
368	Removing any single repeated copy leaves the others intact, yielding ISR~$\approx 0$.
369	
370	\paragraph{Example (Prolog).}
371	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
372	\begin{verbatim}
373	penguin(fido).
374	penguin(fido).
375	penguin(fido).
376	... (repeated to 512 tokens)
377	query(penguin(fido)).
378	\end{verbatim}
379	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
380	
381	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
382	ISR~$\approx 0$. Each copy is individually redundant since identical copies remain after removal.
383	ISR correctly diagnoses this degeneration.
384	
385	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
386	Observed in FOL exec-only seed~43 (87\% of outputs hit max completion length; PR~$=13$\%, ISR~$=0$).
387	
388	
389	\subsection{Complexity-without-Utility}
390	
391	\paragraph{Definition.}
392	The formalization contains syntactically complex premises (nested quantifiers, multi-arity predicates) that are semantically vacuous: none individually necessary for the proof.
393	The complexity inflates surface metrics (formula length, nesting depth) without contributing to logical derivation.
394	
395	\paragraph{Example (FOL).}
396	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
397	{\small\begin{verbatim}
398	Premises:
399	  all x all y (R(x,y) ->
400	    exists z (S(x,z) & T(z,y))).
401	  all x (P(x) -> Q(x)).
402	  P(a).            % only this needed
403	Conclusion: Q(a).
404	\end{verbatim}}
405	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
406	
407	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
408	ISR correctly identifies the vacuous premises (ISR~$=0$ for each), pulling down the mean ISR.
409	This is the mode ISR is best calibrated for.
410	
411	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
412	Low. Observed in Random~$\beta{=}0.1$ seed~42 at 3.8\% (3/79).
413	Not observed under ISR reward (ISR's gradient discourages zero-ISR premises).
414	
415	
416	\subsection{Contradiction Explosion}
417	
418	\paragraph{Definition.}
419	The component count grows unboundedly over training.
420	Excess premises introduce contradictions or redundancies that trivially entail any conclusion via \emph{ex falso quodlibet}.
421	Individual premises may register as ISR~$=0$ (removable without breaking the proof), but the sheer count obscures the underlying triviality.
422	
423	\paragraph{Example (FOL, 1000-step exec-only).}
424	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
425	{\small\begin{verbatim}
426	Premises:
427	  c1. c2. c3. ... c18. % 18.55 mean
428	  % conclusion embedded among premises
429	Conclusion: Q(a).
430	\end{verbatim}}
431	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
432	
433	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
434	ISR~$\approx 0$ (at 1000 steps: ISR~$=0.003$, with 94.8\% of proved samples having ISR~$=0$).
435	
436	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
437	Observed in extended exec-only training (seed~42, 1000 steps).
438	Mean component count grows from ${\sim}6$ (500 steps) to 18.55 (1000 steps).
439	
440	
441	\subsection{Query-as-Fact Gaming}
442	
443	\paragraph{Definition.}
444	The model converts the natural language conclusion into a snake\_case atom and includes it as both a ground fact and the query.
445	Prolog trivially proves the query by unifying with the fact.
446	This mode evades text-match classifiers designed for CE/TS because the generated atom bears no surface resemblance to the original conclusion text.
447	
448	\paragraph{Example (Prolog).}
449	\smallskip\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}
450	\begin{verbatim}
451	% NL: "Fido is happy."
452	% Model output:
453	fido_is_happy.
454	:- fido_is_happy.     % query = fact
455	\end{verbatim}
456	\noindent\rule{\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\smallskip
457	
458	\paragraph{ISR interaction.}
459	ISR~$\approx 0.9$--$1.0$.
460	The single fact is trivially necessary (its removal breaks the query), inflating ISR.
461	Functionally equivalent to TS but with a distinct surface mechanism.
462	
463	\paragraph{Prevalence.}
464	Observed in Prolog exec+STA seed~44 (PR~$=0.97$, ISR~$=0.912$).
465	95/97 proved samples have $n_{\text{necessary}}=1$, confirming functional tautology despite the strict classifier reporting 100\% non-hacking.

676	\subsection{$O(1)$ vs.\ $O(n)$ ISR Ablation}
677	\label{app:o1-ablation}
678	
679	All main experiments (\S\ref{sec:diagnostic}--\ref{sec:crossdomain}) use $O(n)$ full LOO ISR during both training and evaluation; the $O(1)$ binary variant described below exists as an alternative implementation (\texttt{execution\_fast\_sta}) but is used only in the ablation reported here.
680	The two signals measure fundamentally different properties, not approximations of the same quantity.
681	The binary signal tests \emph{collective} premise necessity: $\text{fast\_ISR}(x) = 1$ if $x$ is proved and the conclusion is not provable under empty premises (tautology check), $0$ otherwise.
682	LOO tests \emph{individual} premise necessity: each component is removed in turn, and ISR reflects the fraction whose removal causes proof failure.
683	
684	\paragraph{Evaluation-time comparison.}
685	Across 402 proved samples from all ProofFOL conditions, the $O(1)$ binary signal is 1.0 for every sample: non-tautological conclusions cannot be proved without any premises, so the collective necessity test is trivially satisfied.
686	This renders $O(1)$ unable to distinguish hacking from genuine formalizations: both pass the empty-premise check.
687	By contrast, $O(n)$ LOO produces a clear separation: exec-only models average $\text{ISR}^*{=}0.046$ ($n{=}234$) while ISR-trained models average $\text{ISR}^*{=}0.183$ ($n{=}168$), because hacking constructions contain individually redundant components that LOO detects.
688	Of 179 disagreements between the two signals, all are false positives ($O(1){=}1$, LOO${=}0$): the binary check approves formalizations that LOO correctly identifies as structurally degenerate.
689	
690	\paragraph{Training-time ablation.}
691	As a training reward ($r = 0.5\,r_{\text{exec}} + 0.5\,\text{fast\_ISR}$, seed~42, 500 steps), the $O(1)$ variant achieves $\text{PR}{=}63\%$ with $\text{ISR}^*{=}0.007$, functionally zero, compared to $\text{ISR}^*{=}0.132$ under exec-only and $\text{ISR}^*{=}0.42$ under $O(n)$ ISR $\beta{=}0.1$.
692	The tautology check depresses prove rate by 30\,pp relative to exec-only ($63\%$ vs.\ $93\%$) by penalizing formalizations where the conclusion is provable without premises, but this constraint provides no gradient toward individual component necessity; the model is free to degenerate as long as it avoids trivial tautologies.
693	That even the fine-grained $O(n)$ LOO signal, which \emph{does} provide per-component necessity feedback, is ultimately gamed (\S\ref{sec:goodhart}) underscores that the problem lies in reward optimization itself, not in signal granularity.
694	
695	\paragraph{Computational overhead.}
696	Full LOO requires $n{+}1$ solver calls per sample (mean $n{\approx}6$ for FOLIO).
697	With Prover9 at ${\sim}5$\,ms per call, STA evaluation adds ${\sim}55$\,ms per sample versus ${\sim}4$\,s for LLM inference (${<}1\%$ overhead).
698	The $O(1)$ binary check adds a single solver call per proved sample (${<}2\%$ per training step), but the evaluation above shows this saving comes at the cost of all diagnostic power.

702	
703	We applied the identical GRPO configuration (Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams}) to LLaMA-3.2-3B~\citep{grattafiori2024llama} (3B; exec-only, seed~42, $K{=}4$, FOLIO).

their non-hacking rates may be overestimated.\nRandom $\\beta{=}0.1$ does not produce PG by mechanism (no ISR optimization pressure); seed~42 audit confirmed PG${=}0$.\n\n\\paragraph{TS false-negative correction (TSb obfuscated tautology).}\nISR $\\beta{=}0.1$ seed~44 contained 12 samples with $n_{\\text{prem}}{=}1$ classified as non-hacking by the text matcher.\nManual inspection confirmed all 12 are logically equivalent to TS but evade text matching through vacuous quantifier wrapping ($11/12$) or structural simplification ($1/12$).\nWe classify these as \\textbf{TSb} (obfuscated tautology), distinct from text-match-detectable TSa.\n$10/12$ achieve $\\text{ISR}{\\geq}0.83$, indicating simultaneous gaming of execution reward, ISR metric, and TS classifier: triple gaming (\\S\\ref{sec:taxonomy}).\nAfter correction, ISR s44 non-hacking${=}0$; total TS = TSa (77 text-match) + TSb (12 obfuscated) = 89.\n\n\\paragraph{Impact on non-hacking-rate comparison.}\nAfter PG reclassification and TS false-negative correction: ISR non-hacking $= 14/231 = 6.06\\%$ vs.\\ random non-hacking $= 25/262 = 9.5\\%$ (Fisher $p{=}0.182$, n.s.; Wilson 95\\% CI: ISR [3.6\\%, 9.9\\%], Random [6.5\\%, 13.7\\%]).\nThe direction is consistent with ISR-amplified gaming modes (TS, PG) displacing non-hacking output, but the effect is not statistically significant at the conventional $\\alpha{=}0.05$ threshold.\n\n\\subsection{CE --- Conclusion Embedding}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe conclusion appears verbatim (or with trivial variable renaming) as one premise among multiple premises.\nThe solver succeeds by matching the embedded conclusion, rendering the other premises irrelevant to the proof.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x (bird(x) -> flies(x)).\n  bird(tweety).\n  flies(tweety).  % conclusion embedded\nConclusion: flies(tweety).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nVariable. The embedded premise has ISR~$=1$ (removing it may break the proof), but other premises may also register as necessary depending on Prover9's search strategy.\nISR does not distinguish trivial from substantive necessity.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nConclusion embedding in some form (CE multi-premise or TS single-premise) is GRPO-universal at 68--97\\% across all RL conditions.\nUnder exec-only and Random reward, it manifests as CE (71--93\\%); under ISR reward, it shifts to TS (70.6\\% pooled).\n\n\n\\subsection{TSa --- Conclusion Duplication (Standard)}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe entire formalization collapses to a single premise whose content is identical (text-match detectable) to the conclusion.\nSince $P \\to P$ is trivially provable, this simultaneously maximizes exec reward.\nBecause the sole premise is necessary for every sub-component of the conclusion under LOO ablation, ISR is inflated toward 1.0.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).\nConclusion:\n  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0.8$--$1.0$.\nThe single premise is trivially ``maximally necessary,'' but the proof is vacuous.\nTSa games both exec reward and ISR reward; TSb extends this to triple gaming by additionally evading the text-match classifier.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nTSa (text-match detectable): 64.5\\% pooled (149/231) under ISR~$\\beta{=}0.1$ (s42: 16.0\\%, s43: 96.7\\%, s44: 86.5\\%).\nCombined TS (TSa + TSb): 70.6\\% (163/231; s43 TSa${=}59$ + TSb${=}2$; includes s44 FN correction).\nRandom~$\\beta{=}0.1$ pooled: 13.7\\% (36/262, all TSa; driven by seed~43 at 38.3\\%, seeds~42 and~44 at 0\\%).\n\n\n\\subsection{TSb --- Obfuscated Tautology}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nA single-premise formalization logically equivalent to TSa (premise = conclusion) but with surface-form differences that evade the text-match TS classifier.\nThe model achieves this through vacuous quantifier wrapping ($11/12$ instances) or structural simplification ($1/12$).\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL, vacuous quantifier wrapping).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x all y ((A(x)&B(y))->(C(x)|D(y))).\nConclusion:\n  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\nThe premise introduces a vacuous second variable $y$, immediately unified with $x$; the formulas are logically equivalent but differ textually.\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\n$\\text{ISR}{\\geq}0.83$ ($10/12$ samples).\nTSb simultaneously games three objectives: execution reward ($r_{\\text{exec}}{=}1$ via tautological proof), ISR metric (single necessary premise yields high ISR), and the text-match TS classifier (surface-form difference evades detection).\nThis triple gaming makes TSb the most complete Goodhart case we observe.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nFound primarily in ISR $\\beta{=}0.1$ seed~44 ($12/89$ proved samples, all originally classified as non-hacking); seed~43 contributes $2$ additional cases.\nFunctional cross-validation via $n_{\\text{necessary}}{=}1$ exposed the misclassification.\n\n\n\\subsection{Tautological Collapse}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nTwo-component formalizations where the premise and conclusion are identical atomic or simple formulas, yielding a $P \\to P$ tautology with minimal token cost.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises: p(a).\nConclusion: p(a).\n\\end{verbatim}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$=1.0$ (the sole premise is necessary).\nDistinguished from TS by having low component count ($n_{\\text{comp}}\\approx 2$) and atomic formulas rather than complex sub-structured conclusions.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nDominant in FOL exec-only seed~44 (PR~$=0.98$, mean $n_{\\text{comp}}=2.04$, 100\\% TS by strict classifier).\nAlso observed in Prolog exec+STA seed~42 (100\\% TS, PR~$=0.50$).\n\n\n\\subsection{Repetition Collapse}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe model generates identical premises or clauses repeated until the maximum completion length, producing a degenerate output with high token count but no logical diversity.\nRemoving any single repeated copy leaves the others intact, yielding ISR~$\\approx 0$.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (Prolog).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n\\begin{verbatim}\npenguin(fido).\npenguin(fido).\npenguin(fido).\n... (repeated to 512 tokens)\nquery(penguin(fido)).\n\\end{verbatim}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0$. Each copy is individually redundant since identical copies remain after removal.\nISR correctly diagnoses this degeneration.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nObserved in FOL exec-only seed~43 (87\\% of outputs hit max completion length; PR~$=13$\\%, ISR~$=0$).\n\n\n\\subsection{Complexity-without-Utility}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe formalization contains syntactically complex premises (nested quantifiers, multi-arity predicates) that are semantically vacuous: none individually necessary for the proof.\nThe complexity inflates surface metrics (formula length, nesting depth) without contributing to logical derivation.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x all y (R(x,y) ->\n    exists z (S(x,z) & T(z,y))).\n  all x (P(x) -> Q(x)).\n  P(a).            % only this needed\nConclusion: Q(a).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR correctly identifies the vacuous premises (ISR~$=0$ for each), pulling down the mean ISR.\nThis is the mode ISR is best calibrated for.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nLow. Observed in Random~$\\beta{=}0.1$ seed~42 at 3.8\\% (3/79).\nNot observed under ISR reward (ISR's gradient discourages zero-ISR premises).\n\n\n\\subsection{Contradiction Explosion}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe component count grows unboundedly over training.\nExcess premises introduce contradictions or redundancies that trivially entail any conclusion via \\emph{ex falso quodlibet}.\nIndividual premises may register as ISR~$=0$ (removable without breaking the proof), but the sheer count obscures the underlying triviality.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL, 1000-step exec-only).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  c1. c2. c3. ... c18. % 18.55 mean\n  % conclusion embedded among premises\nConclusion: Q(a).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0$ (at 1000 steps: ISR~$=0.003$, with 94.8\\% of proved samples having ISR~$=0$).\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nObserved in extended exec-only training (seed~42, 1000 steps).\nMean component count grows from ${\\sim}6$ (500 steps) to 18.55 (1000 steps).\n\n\n\\subsection{Query-as-Fact Gaming}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe model converts the natural language conclusion into a snake\\_case atom and includes it as both a ground fact and the query.\nProlog trivially proves the query by unifying with the fact.\nThis mode evades text-match classifiers designed for CE/TS because the generated atom bears no surface resemblance to the original conclusion text.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (Prolog).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n\\begin{verbatim}\n% NL: \"Fido is happy.\"\n% Model output:\nfido_is_happy.\n:- fido_is_happy.     % query = fact\n\\end{verbatim}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0.9$--$1.0$.\nThe single fact is trivially necessary (its removal breaks the query), inflating ISR.\nFunctionally equivalent to TS but with a distinct surface mechanism.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nObserved in Prolog exec+STA seed~44 (PR~$=0.97$, ISR~$=0.912$).\n95/97 proved samples have $n_{\\text{necessary}}=1$, confirming functional tautology despite the strict classifier reporting 100\\% non-hacking.\n\n\n\\section{Per-Seed Detailed total TS = TSa (77 text-match) + TSb (12 obfuscated) = 89.\n\n\\paragraph{Impact on non-hacking-rate comparison.}\nAfter PG reclassification and TS false-negative correction: ISR non-hacking $= 14/231 = 6.06\\%$ vs.\\ random non-hacking $= 25/262 = 9.5\\%$ (Fisher $p{=}0.182$, n.s.; Wilson 95\\% CI: ISR [3.6\\%, 9.9\\%], Random [6.5\\%, 13.7\\%]).\nThe direction is consistent with ISR-amplified gaming modes (TS, PG) displacing non-hacking output, but the effect is not statistically significant at the conventional $\\alpha{=}0.05$ threshold.\n\n\\subsection{CE --- Conclusion Embedding}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe conclusion appears verbatim (or with trivial variable renaming) as one premise among multiple premises.\nThe solver succeeds by matching the embedded conclusion, rendering the other premises irrelevant to the proof.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x (bird(x) -> flies(x)).\n  bird(tweety).\n  flies(tweety).  % conclusion embedded\nConclusion: flies(tweety).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nVariable. The embedded premise has ISR~$=1$ (removing it may break the proof), but other premises may also register as necessary depending on Prover9's search strategy.\nISR does not distinguish trivial from substantive necessity.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nConclusion embedding in some form (CE multi-premise or TS single-premise) is GRPO-universal at 68--97\\% across all RL conditions.\nUnder exec-only and Random reward, it manifests as CE (71--93\\%); under ISR reward, it shifts to TS (70.6\\% pooled).\n\n\n\\subsection{TSa --- Conclusion Duplication (Standard)}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe entire formalization collapses to a single premise whose content is identical (text-match detectable) to the conclusion.\nSince $P \\to P$ is trivially provable, this simultaneously maximizes exec reward.\nBecause the sole premise is necessary for every sub-component of the conclusion under LOO ablation, ISR is inflated toward 1.0.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).\nConclusion:\n  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0.8$--$1.0$.\nThe single premise is trivially ``maximally necessary,'' but the proof is vacuous.\nTSa games both exec reward and ISR reward; TSb extends this to triple gaming by additionally evading the text-match classifier.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nTSa (text-match detectable): 64.5\\% pooled (149/231) under ISR~$\\beta{=}0.1$ (s42: 16.0\\%, s43: 96.7\\%, s44: 86.5\\%).\nCombined TS (TSa + TSb): 70.6\\% (163/231; s43 TSa${=}59$ + TSb${=}2$; includes s44 FN correction).\nRandom~$\\beta{=}0.1$ pooled: 13.7\\% (36/262, all TSa; driven by seed~43 at 38.3\\%, seeds~42 and~44 at 0\\%).\n\n\n\\subsection{TSb --- Obfuscated Tautology}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nA single-premise formalization logically equivalent to TSa (premise = conclusion) but with surface-form differences that evade the text-match TS classifier.\nThe model achieves this through vacuous quantifier wrapping ($11/12$ instances) or structural simplification ($1/12$).\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL, vacuous quantifier wrapping).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x all y ((A(x)&B(y))->(C(x)|D(y))).\nConclusion:\n  all x ((A(x)&B(x))->(C(x)|D(x))).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\nThe premise introduces a vacuous second variable $y$, immediately unified with $x$; the formulas are logically equivalent but differ textually.\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\n$\\text{ISR}{\\geq}0.83$ ($10/12$ samples).\nTSb simultaneously games three objectives: execution reward ($r_{\\text{exec}}{=}1$ via tautological proof), ISR metric (single necessary premise yields high ISR), and the text-match TS classifier (surface-form difference evades detection).\nThis triple gaming makes TSb the most complete Goodhart case we observe.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nFound primarily in ISR $\\beta{=}0.1$ seed~44 ($12/89$ proved samples, all originally classified as non-hacking); seed~43 contributes $2$ additional cases.\nFunctional cross-validation via $n_{\\text{necessary}}{=}1$ exposed the misclassification.\n\n\n\\subsection{Tautological Collapse}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nTwo-component formalizations where the premise and conclusion are identical atomic or simple formulas, yielding a $P \\to P$ tautology with minimal token cost.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises: p(a).\nConclusion: p(a).\n\\end{verbatim}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$=1.0$ (the sole premise is necessary).\nDistinguished from TS by having low component count ($n_{\\text{comp}}\\approx 2$) and atomic formulas rather than complex sub-structured conclusions.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nDominant in FOL exec-only seed~44 (PR~$=0.98$, mean $n_{\\text{comp}}=2.04$, 100\\% TS by strict classifier).\nAlso observed in Prolog exec+STA seed~42 (100\\% TS, PR~$=0.50$).\n\n\n\\subsection{Repetition Collapse}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe model generates identical premises or clauses repeated until the maximum completion length, producing a degenerate output with high token count but no logical diversity.\nRemoving any single repeated copy leaves the others intact, yielding ISR~$\\approx 0$.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (Prolog).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n\\begin{verbatim}\npenguin(fido).\npenguin(fido).\npenguin(fido).\n... (repeated to 512 tokens)\nquery(penguin(fido)).\n\\end{verbatim}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0$. Each copy is individually redundant since identical copies remain after removal.\nISR correctly diagnoses this degeneration.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nObserved in FOL exec-only seed~43 (87\\% of outputs hit max completion length; PR~$=13$\\%, ISR~$=0$).\n\n\n\\subsection{Complexity-without-Utility}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe formalization contains syntactically complex premises (nested quantifiers, multi-arity predicates) that are semantically vacuous: none individually necessary for the proof.\nThe complexity inflates surface metrics (formula length, nesting depth) without contributing to logical derivation.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  all x all y (R(x,y) ->\n    exists z (S(x,z) & T(z,y))).\n  all x (P(x) -> Q(x)).\n  P(a).            % only this needed\nConclusion: Q(a).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR correctly identifies the vacuous premises (ISR~$=0$ for each), pulling down the mean ISR.\nThis is the mode ISR is best calibrated for.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nLow. Observed in Random~$\\beta{=}0.1$ seed~42 at 3.8\\% (3/79).\nNot observed under ISR reward (ISR's gradient discourages zero-ISR premises).\n\n\n\\subsection{Contradiction Explosion}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe component count grows unboundedly over training.\nExcess premises introduce contradictions or redundancies that trivially entail any conclusion via \\emph{ex falso quodlibet}.\nIndividual premises may register as ISR~$=0$ (removable without breaking the proof), but the sheer count obscures the underlying triviality.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (FOL, 1000-step exec-only).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n{\\small\\begin{verbatim}\nPremises:\n  c1. c2. c3. ... c18. % 18.55 mean\n  % conclusion embedded among premises\nConclusion: Q(a).\n\\end{verbatim}}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0$ (at 1000 steps: ISR~$=0.003$, with 94.8\\% of proved samples having ISR~$=0$).\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nObserved in extended exec-only training (seed~42, 1000 steps).\nMean component count grows from ${\\sim}6$ (500 steps) to 18.55 (1000 steps).\n\n\n\\subsection{Query-as-Fact Gaming}\n\n\\paragraph{Definition.}\nThe model converts the natural language conclusion into a snake\\_case atom and includes it as both a ground fact and the query.\nProlog trivially proves the query by unifying with the fact.\nThis mode evades text-match classifiers designed for CE/TS because the generated atom bears no surface resemblance to the original conclusion text.\n\n\\paragraph{Example (Prolog).}\n\\smallskip\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\n\\begin{verbatim}\n% NL: \"Fido is happy.\"\n% Model output:\nfido_is_happy.\n:- fido_is_happy.     % query = fact\n\\end{verbatim}\n\\noindent\\rule{\\columnwidth}{0.3pt}\\smallskip\n\n\\paragraph{ISR interaction.}\nISR~$\\approx 0.9$--$1.0$.\nThe single fact is trivially necessary (its removal breaks the query), inflating ISR.\nFunctionally equivalent to TS but with a distinct surface mechanism.\n\n\\paragraph{Prevalence.}\nObserved in Prolog exec+STA seed~44 (PR~$=0.97$, ISR~$=0.912$).\n95/97 proved samples have $n_{\\text{necessary}}=1$, confirming functional tautology despite the strict classifier reporting 100\\% non-hacking.\n\n\n\\section{Per-Seed Detailed
