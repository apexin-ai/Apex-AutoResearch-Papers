% Appendix E.1: DocRED Ridge Regression (BCS directionality under multicollinearity)

Ridge regression (GCV, $\lambda = 0.001$) on the 110 DocRED batches confirms
the negative direction of the BCS--accuracy relationship even after
regularizing for the severe BCS--centrality multicollinearity
(VIF\,$\approx$\,50.9).
The standardized BCS coefficient is $\hat{\beta}_{\textsc{bcs}} = -1.53$
($\hat{\beta}_{\text{centrality}} = 0.78$, $R^2 = 0.594$).
An analytic derivation shows that $\hat{\beta}_{\textsc{bcs}} < 0$ for
\emph{all} $\lambda \geq 0$: the numerator of the closed-form ridge
solution, $-(1{+}\lambda) \times 0.760 + 0.727$, is negative at
$\lambda{=}0$ and monotonically decreasing thereafter.
Given the shrinkage bias inherent to ridge estimation, we interpret only
the sign---not the magnitude---of the BCS coefficient as directional
evidence that higher schema confusability degrades extraction accuracy,
independent of degree centrality.
