We additionally conducted a permutation-based analysis of the Count$\times$BCS interaction.
Within each count level (8 and 16 types), we randomly permuted the BCS group assignments (low/high, split at the within-level median) across 10,000 iterations.
The observed interaction was -0.48\,pp, and the permutation test for $H_0$: interaction $= 0$ yielded $p = 0.914$, confirming the observed interaction is indistinguishable from zero.
The permutation null distribution (SD = 4.26\,pp) reflects inherent variability with $n \approx$ 12--13 batches per cell.
While the permutation TOST ($p = 0.285$) does not reach significance at $\alpha = 0.05$ due to limited cell sizes, the point estimate of 0.48\,pp is far below the 3\,pp equivalence margin, and the non-significant interaction ($p = 0.914$) corroborates the parametric TOST finding on the linear scale ($p = 0.003$).
