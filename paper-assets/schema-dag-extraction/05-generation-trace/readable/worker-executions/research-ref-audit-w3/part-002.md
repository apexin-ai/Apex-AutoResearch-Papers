## Research Focus

The paper addresses the challenge that humans can recognize tens of thousands of object classes and scenes, while no computer vision algorithm at that time had been tested at this scale, presenting a study of large scale categorization with more than 10,000 image classes.

## Key Findings

The research found that a) computational issues become crucial in algorithm design; b) conventional wisdom from a couple of hundred image categories on relative performance of different classifiers does not necessarily hold when the number of categories increases.

With such large numbers of categories, there is a shift in the difficulty of discriminating between them as the categories sample the semantic space more densely, an effect that was measured in this previously unexplored scale of experiments.
