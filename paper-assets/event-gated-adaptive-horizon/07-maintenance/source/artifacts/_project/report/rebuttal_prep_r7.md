# Rebuttal Preparation — Round 7 Questions

## Q2: Class proportions and AUROC validity

The n=36 evaluation comprises 9 CIVO-positive and 27 non-CIVO configurations (25%/75% split), stated in Section 1 (Contributions, item 1), Section 4.2 (F2 paragraph), and Appendix D (Section "Expanded ROC Analysis," experiment composition paragraph). The class imbalance is addressed in three ways. First, the bootstrap CI uses **stratified resampling** (10,000 resamples preserving the 9/27 class ratio; Appendix D, Section D.3), ensuring the CI is not distorted by majority-class oversampling. Second, we report class-ratio-sensitive metrics alongside AUROC: F1=1.000, precision=1.000, recall=1.000 (Figure 4 caption; Appendix D). Third, leave-one-environment-out (LOEO) cross-validation (Table 6, Appendix D.5) and leave-one-config-out (LOCO) cross-validation (Appendix D.6) both achieve 100% accuracy across all folds, confirming robustness to fold-level class ratio variation. The separation gap between the lowest CIVO-positive NVE (3.78x, 008e_v3 s2 pre-correction) and the highest non-CIVO NVE (1.88x, exp_057) is substantial---no configuration falls in the [1.88x, 3.78x] interval---making AUROC insensitive to class proportions in this regime of perfect rank separation.

## Q3: Code correction impact on classification

Appendix I (Section "Code Corrections and Version History") documents this fully. The bug affected two experiment groups: 008e_v3 (dt=1 imagination) and 008f s0 (no dt-embedding control), comprising 5 pre-correction seeds re-run across 5 post-correction seeds. Classification labels changed for **2 of 5** 008e_v3 seeds: s2 changed from CIVO to non-CIVO, s3 changed from CIVO to non-CIVO (Summary statistics paragraph, Appendix I). The 008f s0 classification was **unchanged** (non-CIVO in both versions). AUROC=1.000 holds both before and after correction: the pre-correction 008e_v3 s2/s3 had NVE of 3.78x and 4.02x respectively, which fall on the CIVO side of the 2.0x threshold regardless (Table 10, Appendix I). The correction **strengthens** conclusions: the dt=1 imagination mitigation achieves 0/4 CIVO under corrected code vs. 2/4 under buggy code. Removing these two entries entirely (n=34, 7 CIVO-positive) does not change AUROC, as the lowest remaining CIVO-positive NVE (4.12x, 008d s1) far exceeds the 2.0x threshold (Appendix D, code-version note paragraph).

## Q4: Evidence for Figure 1 Steps 3->4 and 4->1

We acknowledge the evidence hierarchy explicitly in the Figure 1 caption (Section 1): Steps 1->2 (reward prediction gap) and 2->3 (critic overestimation) are labeled **"observed"** with direct ablation and measurement support (Section 4.2, Table 3 in Appendix C). Step 3->4 (actor exploitation of overestimated regions) is labeled **"inferred"** from two lines of evidence: (a) the random-policy decomposition (Appendix C, CIVO Mechanism subsection) shows 36% of the CIVO effect is dynamics bias alone, while the trained actor amplifies this to the full effect (2.90x vs 1.68x ratio), establishing that actor behavior compounds the bias; (b) entropy dynamics (Appendix C, "Actor entropy dynamics under CIVO" paragraph) show evaluation collapse precedes entropy explosion by ~35K steps, consistent with delusional critic gradients destabilizing the policy. Step 4->1 (reinforced state-distribution skew) is labeled **"hypothesized"**---we do not have direct evidence for the feedback loop closing, and we state this transparently. The causal ordering evidence is correlational (n=2 seeds, temporal lead ~10K steps, r=0.81, p=0.002; Section 4.2, temporal ordering paragraph). Establishing full causal closure would require interventional experiments (e.g., freezing the actor mid-training), which we identify as future work.

## Q5: Diagnostic transferability beyond DreamerV3

The paper's scope is explicitly DreamerV3 (Section 6/Conclusion): "Our analysis covers proprioceptive locomotion and simulated manipulation but not pixels, Atari, or real robots; generalization to other architectures [IRIS] and to learned temporal gates remains open." The NVE diagnostic and CIVO mechanism are grounded in DreamerV3-specific properties: pure-imagination actor-critic training (no real-data value anchoring), RSSM latent dynamics, and dual-critic convergence (rho > 0.999, Section 4.5). The online detector (Section 4.6, Algorithm 1 in Appendix) monitors value-to-evaluation ratio for superlinear growth---a metric that is architecture-agnostic in principle but validated only on DreamerV3 (n=14 runs, three environments; TPR 5/5, FPR 2/9). Generalization to IRIS (discrete-token world model) and TD-MPC2 (MPC-based, which we argue is structurally immune per Appendix E) requires dedicated experiments and is explicitly deferred. The Broader Impact Statement further scopes the safeguards as "specific to DreamerV3's RSSM." We view cross-architecture validation as the highest-priority extension but do not overclaim transferability from the current evidence base.
