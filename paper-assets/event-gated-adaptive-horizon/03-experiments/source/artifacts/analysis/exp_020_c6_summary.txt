## exp_020 — C6 Confirmed: Trained dt-emb is Necessary for Pathway B Collapse

**Configuration:** Vanilla DreamerV3 (no gate mechanism, no dt-embedding, no subsample), seed=2, H=15, Walker Walk, 105K steps. Server bjb1-45299 cuda:0.

**Results:** eval_return trajectory: 41→106→325→354→498→534→689→657→619→596→837. Peak 837@105K. No collapse detected. The 75K-95K dip (689→596) is normal training variance with full recovery to new high at 105K.

**Control comparison (008e_v3_s2):** Same seed=2, same environment, but with trained dt-embedding + subsample ON + dt=1 imagination + H=15. That experiment collapsed irreversibly: 715@85K→137@105K (NVE=3.78×). The only architectural difference is the presence of a trained dt-embedding.

**Conclusion (C6):** Trained dt-embedding is a necessary condition for Pathway B (Imagination-Reality Divergence) collapse. Without trained dt-emb, DreamerV3 seed=2 trains stably to 837 eval return. With trained dt-emb, the same seed collapses to 137. This rules out the alternative hypothesis that Pathway B is an inherent DreamerV3 fragility independent of temporal abstraction.
