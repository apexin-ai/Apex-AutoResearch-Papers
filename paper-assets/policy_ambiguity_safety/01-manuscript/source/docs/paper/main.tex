\section*{Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

Our stimulus set uses synthetic policy clauses inspired by real-world terms of service; no proprietary policy text is reproduced verbatim. The real-world prevalence audit (Appendix~AH) analyzes publicly available terms-of-service documents and reports only aggregate statistics, with no personally identifiable information. Our experiments reveal that policy ambiguity can systematically induce safety violations in LLM agents; we disclose these findings to support safer deployment practices rather than to enable adversarial exploitation. Human annotators who participated in the inter-annotator agreement validation were co-authors of this work and were not recruited through crowdsourcing platforms.
