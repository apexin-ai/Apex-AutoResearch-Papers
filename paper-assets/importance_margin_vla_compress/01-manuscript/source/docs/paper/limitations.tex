\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The six architectures differ in backbone, encoder, and scale beyond the connector, so the observed pruning tolerance cannot be causally attributed to the connector alone. All evaluations use LIBERO simulation; real-robot transfer remains untested. Most conditions use a single seed ($\pi_{0.5}$ Long: $n{=}5$, s.d.\ ${\leq}$2.7\,pp); broader multi-seed coverage would tighten confidence intervals.
