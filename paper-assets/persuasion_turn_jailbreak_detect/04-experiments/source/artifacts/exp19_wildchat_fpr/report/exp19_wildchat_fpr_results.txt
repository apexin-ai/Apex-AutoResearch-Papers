WildChat Hard Negatives FPR Evaluation (exp19)

Dataset: 226 benign conversations from WildChat (filtered: multi-turn, high drift)
Average turns per conversation: 7.4
Checkpoints: exp17 (DeBERTa-v3-base, 1000-sample dataset)

FPR (False Positive Rate = % benign incorrectly classified as jailbreak):

vanilla:

Key finding: All three model variants have unacceptably high FPR on real-world WildChat benign conversations, confirming significant distribution shift between LLM-generated training benign data and real user conversations. 9-class is worst (68%), likely because the multi-task heads overfit to stylistic patterns of the training data.
