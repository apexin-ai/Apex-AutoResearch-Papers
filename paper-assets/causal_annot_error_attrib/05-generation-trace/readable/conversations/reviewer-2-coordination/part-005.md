## What Makes a Good Review

**Specificity is essential.** Reviews should identify concrete issues rather than vague criticisms. For example, instead of "X is not clear," explain "Y and Z are missing from the description of X."

**Professional tone matters.** Reviews should be "professional and neutral, if not kind." The guidelines emphasize that reviewers hold power in anonymous settings and should write reviews they'd want to receive themselves.

**Acknowledge author responses.** Reviewers should "at least acknowledge the authors' response" and update assessments when concerns are adequately addressed.

## The Three-Score System (February 2025 onwards)

**Soundness Score:**
This evaluates technical validity. Importantly, "low Soundness scores must be justified by the text of the review." If you rate soundness poorly without identifying specific flaws, your review lacks internal consistency.

**Excitement Score:**
This reflects personal interest—described as "orthogonal to the assessment of the soundness." A reviewer can find work sound but personally unexciting, and vice versa.
