R6 Batch 1A: κ-diversity vs K_eff Regression Comparison
========================================================

Method: Wild Cluster Bootstrap (B=9999, Rademacher, max-p across K positions, BH FDR α=0.05)
15 models, 2 benchmarks × 6 attacks = 12 conditions per K

PREDICTOR SHIFT COMPARISON
--------------------------
  K    κ_div FDR sig    K_eff FDR sig    Mean ρ(κ_div, K_eff)
  3         8/12             3/12              0.9893
  5         7/12             7/12              0.9867
  7         6/12             8/12              0.9855

KEY FINDING: κ-diversity and K_eff are rank-correlated at ρ > 0.985 across
all panel sizes, yet their FDR-significance patterns diverge in OPPOSITE
directions as K grows:
  - κ-diversity: 8 → 7 → 6 (DECREASING)
  - K_eff:       3 → 7 → 8 (INCREASING)

All significant coefficients are positive (none negative) for both predictors.

REBUTTAL IMPLICATION:
K_eff is NOT "dressed up" κ-diversity. Despite near-perfect rank correlation,
the MI-based K_eff formulation increasingly outperforms agreement-based κ-diversity
at larger panel sizes. The predictor shift pattern (few→many significant conditions
as K grows) is UNIQUE to K_eff — κ-diversity shows the opposite trend. This
demonstrates that K_eff captures information-theoretic structure (conditional
independence via MI) that goes beyond pairwise agreement.
