\subsection{Weakest-Link Dominance at \texorpdfstring{$K{=}3$}{K=3}}
\label{sec:dominance}

Table~\ref{tab:dominance} reports the log-log OLS regression (Equation~\ref{eq:regression}) for all 12 conditions across the 455 panels ($K{=}3$, 15 judges from Table~\ref{tab:models}).

Under OLS, $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is FDR-significant in all 12/12 conditions (positive in 10, negative in 2 position-bias conditions). $\eta_{\max}$ is significant in 9/12 (exceptions: both score-manipulation conditions and MMLU $\times$ authority bias), consistent with weakest-link monotonicity (\S\ref{sec:threat_model}). Dominance analysis yields 7 co-dominant, 3 $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$-dominant, and 2 $\eta_{\max}$-dominant (both position-bias) conditions (Figure~\ref{fig:dominance_heatmap}, Appendix~\ref{app:extended_analysis}), but the $r{=}0.70$ correlation raises confound concerns (\S\ref{sec:analysis}).

Figure~\ref{fig:robustness} compares five correction methods (Table~\ref{tab:method_comparison}, Appendix~\ref{app:clustered_se}): $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ significance ranges from 0/12 (clustered SE) to 12/12 (OLS), with wild bootstrap at 8/12. Positive direction is preserved in 10/12 conditions across all methods (minimum detectable effect [MDE] $\beta_{\mathrm{std}} {=} 0.14$; Appendix~\ref{app:power_analysis}).

\subsection{Predictor Relevance Across Panel Sizes}
\label{sec:cross_k}

The $K{=}3$ pattern is method-sensitive (Table~\ref{tab:method_comparison}), contrasting with $K{\geq}5$ where $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$'s association is consistent (bootstrap 8/12; fractional logit 11--12/12). We repeat the bootstrap at $K \in \{3, 5, 7\}$ with max-$p$ correction.

Meta-analytic pooling \citep{dersimonian1986meta} quantifies trajectories independent of significance thresholds. $\eta_{\max}$'s pooled $\hat{\beta}_{\mathrm{std}}$ is $0.243$ [$0.163, 0.323$] at $K{=}3$ but drops to $0.091$ [$-0.031, 0.212$] at $K{=}5$ and $0.086$ [$-0.039, 0.211$] at $K{=}7$, becoming non-significant at $K{\geq}5$ (CI crosses zero). $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$'s pooled coefficient remains positive ($0.286$--$0.353$, CI excluding zero; Appendix~\ref{app:meta_analytic_details}). Figure~\ref{fig:regime_transition} shows significance counts; per-condition effect sizes with 95\% CIs are in Figure~\ref{fig:forest_plot} (Appendix~\ref{app:effect_size_forest}). The $\eta_{\max}$ decline holds under both $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and $\kappa$-diversity.

Random faults yield $R^2{=}0.730$; adaptive attack reduces predictability ($R^2{=}0.285$ at $K{=}5$) but $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ retains significance (7/12; Appendix~\ref{app:rf_contrast_detail}). The positive $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ coefficient reverses the BFT/Condorcet intuition: under random faults, independence helps (more independent judges $=$ lower joint failure); under adaptive adversaries, diverse panels expose a wider attack surface because the adversary can exploit heterogeneous vulnerabilities that do not overlap across judges.

\subsection{Attack-Type Heterogeneity}
\label{sec:gam_taxonomy}

GAM bootstrap confirms positive $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ association in 10/12 conditions with no magnitude difference between targeted and shared-bias attacks (Appendix~\ref{app:bft_reversal}). The two exceptions are position bias: $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$'s coefficient is negative in both benchmarks ($\hat{\beta}_{\mathrm{std}} = -0.334, -0.372$), indicating that diverse panels are \emph{less} susceptible. Because position bias is a shared vulnerability where all judges exhibit the same directional preference, independent judges provide genuine redundancy, consistent with the Condorcet intuition and the targeted-vs-shared distinction in the attack taxonomy.

\subsection{Aggregation Rules and the Diversity Channel}
\label{sec:aggregation}

Aggregation rules influence predictability but not $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$'s direction (Table~\ref{tab:aggregation}, Appendix~\ref{app:aggregation_details}). Threshold voting yields the highest $R^2$ ($0.745$), while Bayesian calibration nearly neutralizes panel composition effects ($R^2{=}0.136$, $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 5+/1$-$). At $K{=}5$ all methods converge (7/12; Appendix~\ref{app:aggregation_k5}); at $K{=}7$ Bayesian neutralization returns (Appendix~\ref{app:aggregation_k7}). Fractional logit \citep{papke1996econometric} with clustered SE confirms the association: $K_{\mathrm{eff}}$ significance ranges from 9/12 ($K{=}3$) to 12/12 ($K{=}7$), with 98.6\% directional concordance (Appendix~\ref{app:robustness_checks}).
