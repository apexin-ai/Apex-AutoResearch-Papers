# exp-027: MLP + Random Forest Verifiers\n\n## Setup\n- Data: SciERC NER, N=16, seeds=[42, 123, 456]\n- Instances per seed: 551\n- Binary label: instance best-sample F1 > median\n- Instance AUROC: 5-fold stratified CV\n- Selection F1: 5-fold grouped CV (grouped by instance)\n- Features: 5 basic (SJ, FK, LP, EM, VC) or +10 pairwise interactions = 15\n\n## Main Results (3-seed mean +/- std)\n\n| Model | Features | AUROC | Selection F1 |\n|-------|----------|-------|-------------|\n| Best single (EM) | 1 | 0.6476 | 0.6470 (LP) |\n| LR | 5 | 0.6522 +/- 0.0130 | 0.6406 +/- 0.0010 |\n| MLP | 5 | 0.6393 +/- 0.0209 | 0.6377 +/- 0.0031 |\n| RF | 5 | 0.5868 +/- 0.0208 | 0.6133 +/- 0.0120 |\n| MLP | 15 | 0.6322 +/- 0.0165 | 0.6339 +/- 0.0079 |\n| RF | 15 | 0.6043 +/- 0.0314 | 0.6143 +/- 0.0148 |\n| Greedy | - | - | 0.6441 |\n| Oracle | - | - | 0.8231 |\n\n## Key Findings\n\n1. **MLP ~ LR on 5 features** (AUROC delta=-0.0129): nonlinear model does NOT improve over LR. Features are the bottleneck, not model capacity.\n2. **RF underperforms** both LR and MLP (AUROC=0.5868), likely overfitting to noise with limited features.\n3. Interaction features (15-feat): MLP delta=-0.0071, RF delta=+0.0175 - marginal or no gain.\n4. Best supervised AUROC=0.6522 vs best single signal=0.6476 (delta=+0.0046)\n5. Best supervised selection F1=0.6406 vs best unsup=0.6470 (delta=-0.0064)\n\n## Conclusion\n\n**Features are the bottleneck, not model capacity.** MLP and RF fail to improve over simple LR on 5 signals, and even LR barely beats the best single signal (EM). This confirms that the 5 instance-level aggregates (SJ, FK, LP, EM, VC) have limited discriminative information that cannot be unlocked by nonlinear models or interaction terms. Future work should focus on richer features (e.g., per-sample structural patterns, entity-level signals) rather than more complex classifiers.
