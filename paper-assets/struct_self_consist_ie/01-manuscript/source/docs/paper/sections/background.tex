\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Test-time scaling and self-consistency.}\label{sec:yun_contrast}
Self-consistency (SC) \cite{wang2023selfconsistency} and best-of-$N$ with scaling laws \cite{snell2025scaling, brown2024monkeys, gao2023scaling} yield large gains on reasoning; recent extensions include reward-tail-guided search \cite{li2026rewardtail}. Reward-free selectors \cite{taubenfeld2025cisc, kang2025selfcertainty} and trained verifiers \cite{cobbe2021verifiers, lightman2024verify, li2023stepaware} target discrete-answer settings.
SC can fail when consistent outputs are wrong \cite{chen2023twofailures}; free-form \cite{chen2024usc} and atomic \cite{thirukovalluru2024atomic} variants face limits where sub-output agreement does not yield selection gain. Iterative self-verification \cite{shinn2023reflexion} offers an alternative, but our ESJ and multi-round self-consistency (MRSC) results (\S\ref{sec:selection_gap}) show such approaches also fail for NER.
\citet{yun2025price} identify diversity collapse under constrained decoding; we extend their qualitative account with quantitative diagnostics (\S\ref{sec:selection_gap}).

\paragraph{Consistency for structured information extraction (IE).}
LLM-based IE has expanded rapidly \cite{xu2024llmie}, yet reliability under repeated sampling is underexplored.
\citet{yao2026consistency} show consistency correlates with accuracy for NER/RE ($\rho$ up to 0.47); we extend to instance-level analysis: task-level $\rho$ masks instance-level degeneracy, and we decompose failure into degeneracy and LP compression.
Critically, their task-level granularity cannot distinguish instances where scaling helps from those where it harms; our entity-level decomposition addresses this gap (Table~\ref{tab:novelty_comparison}).
\citet{xie2023zeroshot} propose entity voting for zero-shot NER; \citet{xie2024selfimproving} extend this with SC-based self-annotation, reporting gains where zero-shot diversity is high.
Self-verification approaches that prompt LLMs to validate their own extractions improve NER \cite{wang2025gptner} and clinical IE \cite{gero2023selfverification}, but operate on single outputs rather than multi-sample selection.
\citet{wadhwa2023revisiting} benchmark LLM-based RE, confirming that structured outputs pose challenges distinct from free-form generation; our work quantifies these through sample consistency.

\paragraph{Uncertainty estimation and minimum Bayes risk (MBR).}
Our selection instantiates MBR decoding \cite{eikema2022sampling}; \citet{eikema2025structured} find MBR utilities degrade on structured outputs, consistent with our LP compression findings.
LP is well-calibrated at population level \cite{kadavath2022language, tian2023calibration} and motivated as a quality signal by noisy channel models \cite{yee2019noisy}, yet too compressed within instances for selection.
Complementary work on uncertainty for structured prediction \cite{malinin2021uncertainty}, calibrated LMs \cite{desai2020calibration}, semantic uncertainty \cite{kuhn2023semantic}, and conformal prediction \cite{angelopoulos2023conformal} addresses related but orthogonal challenges.

\paragraph{Concurrent work.}
\citet{landesberg2026bon} independently document a correlation-selection gap in best-of-$N$ reasoning: global judge $r{=}0.47$ masks within-prompt $r{=}0.27$ (Table~\ref{tab:novelty_comparison}).
This concurrent discovery validates the gap as cross-domain.
The studies diverge in four respects (Table~\ref{tab:novelty_comparison}):
\textit{(a)~domain}: reasoning (scalar judgments) vs.\ NER/RE (entity sets with partial overlap);
\textit{(b)~failure mechanism}: prompt-level baseline effects vs.\ degeneracy ($12$--$68$\%) and signal alignment collapse ($\alpha{\to}0$), mechanisms specific to structured prediction;
\textit{(c)~granularity}: prompt-level $r$ decomposition vs.\ instance- and entity-level diagnostics ($D$, $G_{\textup{nd}}$, $\alpha$);
\textit{(d)~prescriptive}: no constructive remedy vs.\ entity-level construction with MBR grounding (Proposition~\ref{prop:construction_mbr}).
