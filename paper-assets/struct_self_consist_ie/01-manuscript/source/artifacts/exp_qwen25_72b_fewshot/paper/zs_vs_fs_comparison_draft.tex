\paragraph{Prompting regime does not determine construction success.}
Table~\ref{tab:zs_vs_fs} compares the same 72B model under zero-shot and 3-shot prompting.
Few-shot examples improve greedy F1 on all available datasets (SciERC: $.399 \to .461$; CoNLL: $.712 \to .745$), confirming that demonstrations aid raw extraction quality.
Yet this performance gain does not translate into construction gains: SciERC zero-shot is the \emph{only} configuration where LP-weighted construction yields a significant improvement ($+1.53$\,pp, $p{=}0.008$), while the corresponding few-shot configuration shows no effect ($-0.08$\,pp, n.s.).
The regime that produces higher greedy quality does not produce better self-consistency--based refinement.

\paragraph{Degeneracy and LP alignment jointly gate construction.}
Two conditions must be met for construction to improve over greedy: (i) degeneracy must be low enough to preserve candidate diversity, and (ii) LP must carry sufficient quality signal to weight candidates effectively.
SciERC zero-shot satisfies both (21.0\% degeneracy, LP $\rho{=}0.081$---weak but nonzero), enabling $+1.53$\,pp.
SciERC few-shot has even lower degeneracy (16.6\%) but LP drops to $\rho{=}0.060$, and construction fails ($-0.08$\,pp).
CoNLL few-shot shows the converse: LP recovers to $\rho{=}0.106$ but degeneracy is saturated at 70.0\%, leaving no diverse candidates for aggregation ($+0.03$\,pp, n.s.).
Neither condition alone suffices---this \emph{dual-gating} pattern is consistent across both regimes and all three datasets.
Construction succeeds only in the narrow operating region where degeneracy is low \emph{and} LP retains discriminative power.
