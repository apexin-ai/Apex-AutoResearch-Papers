TEAL vs KL Neuron Overlap Analysis (50% sparsity, LLaMA-3.1-8B, 27 samples)

Overall Jaccard: 0.396 (low overlap)
Divergence ratio: 43.3% of pruned neurons non-overlapping

KL-only pruned neurons:
- Mean |activation|: 0.0588 (1.48x global mean of 0.0398)
- These are high-magnitude neurons KL sacrifices for PPL

TEAL-only pruned neurons:
- Mean KL gate prob: 0.614 (KL wants to KEEP these)
- These are low-magnitude neurons TEAL prunes but KL preserves

Per-layer Jaccard (U-shaped):
- Lowest overlap: L17-L18 (Jaccard ~0.19) - middle layers (knowledge storage)
- Highest overlap: L26-L28 (Jaccard ~0.55) - late layers

Interpretation: KL optimizes for output distribution (PPL) by pruning neurons
whose removal least affects next-token prediction, even if they have high
activation magnitude. TEAL's magnitude-based strategy naturally protects
high-activation knowledge neurons. Middle-layer divergence aligns with
knowledge neuron literature (factual knowledge concentrated in mid layers).

This explains the PPL-downstream disconnect: KL wins PPL but TEAL preserves
knowledge neurons → better MMLU.
