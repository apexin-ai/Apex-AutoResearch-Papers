\section{Generalization and Robustness}
\label{sec:generalization}

We test whether atlas accuracy (68.9--85.9\% four-class; d1--d3: 58.9--81.5\%) reflects genuine statistical regularities across eight generalization axes (five below, three in Appendix).

\subsection{Classifier Comparison and Surface-Text Ablation}
\label{sec:gen:baselines}

Table~\ref{tab:baselines} compares methods across all 9 cells.
Four classifiers (RF, XGBoost, ordinal multilayer perceptron (MLP), prototypical) converge within 0.8\,pp under cross-model transfer (Appendix~\ref{app:learned_estimator}), which confirms a feature-determined accuracy ceiling.

\paragraph{Neural end-to-end baselines.}
DeBERTa-v3-base (LoRA, 53--61\%) and RoBERTa-DCOL (ordinal contrastive, 64.5\%) lag RF by 14--33\,pp; note that RF features access reference-model log-probabilities unavailable to end-to-end pretrained language model (PLM) baselines (\S\ref{sec:discussion}).

\paragraph{Binary detectors and membership inference.}
Perturbation-based (DetectGPT, 28.8\%) and membership-inference (Min-K\% Prob, 33.2\%) methods produce scores with no ordinal resolution (Table~\ref{tab:baselines}); Binoculars d1v2 AUC\,=\,0.50--0.53 vs.\ RF 0.93 (Appendix~\ref{app:detector_baselines}). Binoculars convergence with our pipeline is discussed in \S\ref{sec:discussion}.

\paragraph{Surface-text ablation.}
Single-feature regression on mean perplexity (25.1\%) and zero-shot LLMs (best 36.2\%; Appendix~\ref{app:zeroshot_prompt}) confirm depth signal is inaccessible from surface text.

\subsection{Cross-Strategy Transfer}
\label{sec:gen:crossstrategy}

Cross-strategy transfer reveals a structural barrier: d1--d3 balanced accuracy drops to 45.9\% (four-class: 59.1\%), 7\,pp below cross-model transfer (Table~\ref{tab:cross_strategy}; Appendix~\ref{app:cross_strategy}).
A two-stage pipeline (strategy classifier $\to$ depth classifier) recovers much of this gap (70.6\%, $+$11.5\,pp; oracle: 78.4\%).
Our claims hold most strongly within-strategy or when the decoding configuration is known.

\subsection{Preliminary Cross-Scale Observations (14B)}
\label{sec:gen:crossscale}

At 14B (Qwen2.5-14B), self-reference yields 50.8\% ($2{\times}$ chance, $\mathrm{EDD}_{0.70}{=}0$); cross-reference (Pythia-1.4B) raises $\mathrm{EDD}_{0.70}$ to 2 for nucleus and temperature but reduces accuracy by 6.8\,pp, making reference-model choice a major confound (Appendix~\ref{app:cross_scale_full}).
At matched 1.5B scale, instruction tuning preserves depth signal (Appendix~\ref{app:instruct_1p5b}); cross-domain transfer to arXiv reaches 74.1\% (Appendix~\ref{app:cross_boundary}).

\paragraph{Partial-mixing (accumulation).}
\label{sec:gen:accumulation}
Real-world contamination mixes synthetic and human text.
We test robustness under 25\% and 50\% synthetic mixing across three cells (Table~\ref{tab:accum_auc}).

Per-passage predictions remain composition-independent (${\leq}$2.4\,pp recall shift; Appendix~\ref{app:mixed_depth}).
