## Key Observations

1. **Entropy non-monotonic**: Sharp drop gen_0→gen_1 (2.97→2.17), then flat/slight recovery (2.17→2.27). This explains why entropy is NOT a robust canary at α=0.50 — the drop-then-stabilize pattern breaks lead-lag assumptions.

2. **ECE monotonic rise**: 0.008→0.157, clearly leads distinct_3 decline. ECE is the robust canary (4/5 methods confirm forward causation).

3. **distinct_3 monotonic decline**: 0.817→0.500, saturating toward a floor around gen_10+.

4. **PPL monotonic rise**: 19.83→37.09, steady ~1.1/gen increase.

5. **Comparison with α=0.60**: At α=0.60 both entropy AND ece were robust canaries (4/5 each). At α=0.50, only ece retains robustness. This aligns with the hypothesis that lower contamination weakens entropy signal but ece remains detectable.
