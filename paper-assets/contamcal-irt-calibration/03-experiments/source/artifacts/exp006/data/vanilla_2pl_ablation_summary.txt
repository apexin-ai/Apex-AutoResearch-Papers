Vanilla 2PL Pooled Ablation Results
=====================================

Purpose: Prove gamma has no calibration contribution at pooled level (Simpson's Paradox)

Comparison (same N=13 data, MMLU and ARC):

Benchmark     | 2PL+gamma cal_MAE | Vanilla 2PL cal_MAE | Winner
------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- | ------
MMLU          | 0.0051            | 0.0036              | vanilla_2pl
ARC Challenge | 0.0229            | 0.0117              | vanilla_2pl
GSM8K*        | 0.0062 (N=24)     | 0.0087 (N=21)       | 2pl_gamma (different N, not comparable)

* GSM8K 2pl_gamma rerun with N=21 crashed (chains 3,4 failed), using original N=24 run.

Conclusion: At pooled level, vanilla 2PL matches or beats 2PL+gamma on MMLU and ARC.
This confirms gamma is approximately 0 at aggregate level and supports the hypothesis
that gamma's value emerges only at domain-level analysis (Simpson's Paradox).
