## Core Problem

Benchmark scores are increasingly unreliable because training data may contain semantic paraphrases of test items that evade n-gram decontamination, and RL post-training further conceals contamination signals while amplifying score inflation. While recent work characterizes contamination effects (dose-response curves, detection methods, IRT-based item quality, mitigation strategy failures), no existing method provides a principled statistical framework that takes per-item contamination exposure estimates and outputs calibrated benchmark scores with uncertainty bounds—answering the question 'what would this model's score be if it had never seen the test data?'

## Opportunity / Why Now

Rephrased test items can inflate MMLU accuracy by 8-15 points while evading standard decontamination. RL post-training (GRPO) further amplifies contamination effects: Yang et al. (arXiv 2601.06103) show that GRPO not only resurfaces leaked information but inflates performance on uncontaminated counterparts (GSMPlus, HumanEval), while SFT inflates only contaminated tasks—making RL contamination uniquely dangerous. 'The Emperor's New Clothes in Benchmarking?' (ICML 2025) demonstrates that no existing mitigation strategy (across 20 strategies tested on 10 LLMs and 5 benchmarks) effectively balances fidelity and contamination resistance, motivating post-hoc calibration rather than benchmark modification. PSN-IRT (AAAI 2026) demonstrated that IRT can identify low-quality benchmark items, but does not model contamination explicitly or produce adjusted scores. HiBayES showed hierarchical Bayesian models improve LLM evaluation with proper uncertainty, but ignores contamination. The dose-response curve paper (arXiv 2601.04301) characterizes how contamination scales with model size and dosage, but does not calibrate scores. Self-Critique (arXiv 2510.09259) detects RL contamination with 30% AUC improvement over baselines, but does not adjust scores—and its per-item signals carry substantial noise (dataset-level AUC 0.60-0.70), motivating explicit noise propagation analysis. KDS (ICML 2025) achieves near-perfect correlation with contamination levels but requires white-box fine-tuning access and has not been validated on RL-trained models. The clear gap: a contamination-augmented IRT framework that synthesizes detection signals into per-item exposure estimates and outputs Bayesian-calibrated scores with posterior credible intervals. With EMNLP 2026 explicitly inviting work on contamination and data governance (ARR deadline May 25, 2026), this fills a critical methodological gap.

## Landscape (SOTA & Limitations)

Eight detection paradigms are now recognized: time-based partition, Min-K%++ membership inference (ICLR 2025), Time Travel guided completion (ICLR 2024), ConStat performance gaps (NeurIPS 2024), BIG-Bench canary memorization, Self-Critique entropy collapse for RL contamination (arXiv 2510.09259), hierarchical semantic detection (arXiv 2025, F1=0.76), and Kernel Divergence Score (KDS, ICML 2025) which achieves near-perfect Spearman correlation (0.99) with contamination levels by measuring kernel similarity matrix divergence before/after fine-tuning—though it requires white-box access with LoRA fine-tuning, has O(N²) complexity, and has not been validated post-RL. For score adjustment, DCR (EMNLP 2025) produces adjusted accuracy via fuzzy inference but lacks uncertainty bounds and does not handle RL contamination. Inference-Time Decontamination (EMNLP 2024 Findings) rewrites leaked samples at test time but operates during inference rather than post-hoc. 'The Emperor's New Clothes in Benchmarking?' (ICML 2025) systematically evaluates 20 BDC mitigation strategies across 10 LLMs and 5 benchmarks, finding that no strategy effectively balances fidelity and contamination resistance—strongly motivating post-hoc calibration approaches like ours. The dose-response paper (arXiv 2601.04301, NeurIPS 2025) fits scaling laws across contamination dosage (34M-344M models) but characterizes effects rather than calibrating scores. Yang et al. (arXiv 2601.06103) use the same models we plan to use (Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B) for controlled contamination injection, finding that SFT inflates only contaminated tasks while GRPO inflates uncontaminated counterparts too—validating our experimental setup and providing a natural comparison point, but they characterize effects rather than calibrate scores. For psychometric evaluation, PSN-IRT (AAAI 2026 Oral) applies 4PL IRT with neural networks across 41,871 items on 11 benchmarks but does not model contamination as a covariate and provides point estimates without Bayesian uncertainty. ATLAS (arXiv 2511.04689) applies adaptive testing with IRT for efficient evaluation but does not address contamination. HiBayES (AISI, arXiv 2505.05602) demonstrates hierarchical Bayesian evaluation with credible intervals but ignores contamination. Han et al. (EACL 2026 Findings) quantify contamination in psychometric evaluations via item/evaluation memorization and target score matching, but do not use IRT or produce calibrated scores. The fragility paper (arXiv 2510.02386) shows GRPO conceals contamination signals by collapsing log-probability distributions—Min-K%++ and ConStat fail post-GRPO, but Self-Critique's entropy-based signal survives because it exploits policy collapse rather than probability gaps. The ICML 2025 paper 'How Much Can We Forget about Data Contamination' shows contamination persists in small models following standard scaling laws, making controlled experiments on 1B-3B models tractable and informative. Two concurrent works further validate our approach direction: 'Growing Pains: Extensible and Efficient LLM Benchmarking Via Fixed Parameter Calibration' (arXiv 2604.12843, April 2026) applies multidimensional 2PL IRT with anchor items for efficient LLM benchmarking across 400+ models, predicting full-evaluation performance within 2-3 percentage points using only 100 anchor questions (Spearman ρ ≥ 0.9)—this validates IRT as a rigorous framework for LLM evaluation at scale but does not address contamination, confirming the open gap our work fills. 'Silicon Bureaucracy and AI Test-Oriented Education: Contamination Sensitivity and Score Confidence in LLM Benchmarks' (arXiv 2603.21636, March 2026) proposes a router-worker audit framework that tests contamination sensitivity across 12 LLMs by comparing clean-baseline and noisy-perturbation conditions, finding widespread above-baseline gains under noisy conditions (10/12 models rise above baseline at 8 routers)—this takes a complementary auditing approach that produces contamination flags and credibility assessments rather than calibrated scores, further motivating our Bayesian calibration framework which outputs quantitative score corrections with uncertainty bounds. The clear remaining gap: a principled Bayesian framework that (1) synthesizes multiple contamination signals (including RL-robust ones) into per-item exposure estimates, (2) extends IRT with a contamination covariate to produce calibrated adjusted scores with posterior credible intervals, and (3) explicitly quantifies how noisy detection signals propagate into calibration uncertainty.

## Proposed Approach

We propose ContamCal, a two-stage framework for contamination-aware benchmark score calibration. Stage 1 (Multi-Signal Provenance Mapping): For each model-item pair, we compute contamination exposure scores by combining four complementary signals—(a) embedding-based semantic near-duplicate retrieval (FAISS + sentence transformers) against known training corpora, (b) Min-K%++ membership inference scores, (c) ConStat-style performance gap analysis between original and paraphrased items, and (d) Self-Critique entropy collapse detection for RL-post-trained models. Critically, we use training-free Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) to aggregate signals into a composite per-item contamination exposure score, avoiding the circularity of learned weights that would require labeled contamination ground truth. RRF ranks items by each signal independently and fuses ranks via reciprocal weighting (1/(k+rank)), which is robust to score scale differences across signals and requires no training data. We additionally report results with simple rank averaging and per-signal ablations to verify robustness. We include a signal survival analysis: we systematically evaluate which detection signals remain reliable after SFT vs. after RL post-training, directly addressing the fragility problem (arXiv 2510.02386) by showing that Self-Critique compensates where Min-K%++ and ConStat degrade. We also discuss KDS (ICML 2025) as a high-performing alternative signal but note it requires per-model LoRA fine-tuning (expensive for multi-model evaluation), has O(N²) complexity, and lacks RL robustness validation—making it complementary but not practical as a default signal in our multi-model framework. Stage 2 (Contamination-Augmented Bayesian IRT): We fit a hierarchical Bayesian 4PL IRT model augmented with a contamination covariate: P(correct_ij = 1) = c_j + (d_j - c_j) · σ(a_j(θ_i - b_j) + γ · exposure_ij), where θ_i is model ability, (a_j, b_j, c_j, d_j) are item parameters, exposure_ij is the per-item contamination score from Stage 1, and γ captures the contamination effect. Bayesian inference is performed via Stan's NUTS sampler (primary) with an explicit convergence fallback strategy: if NUTS produces divergent transitions or fails to converge within 24 hours (diagnosed via R-hat > 1.01, bulk/tail ESS checks, or >5% divergent transitions), we fall back to (1) Stan's ADVI (Automatic Differentiation Variational Inference) with fullrank approximation, which provides orders-of-magnitude speedup and has been shown effective for unidimensional IRT models, or (2) PyMC's fullrank ADVI as an alternative backend. ADVI discrimination bias mitigation: Ulitzsch & Nestler (2022) demonstrate that ADVI produces reliable difficulty (b_j) estimates but systematically biased (downward) discrimination (a_j) estimates, with bias severity increasing with model dimensionality. We address this with four mitigations: (i) our model is effectively unidimensional (single θ per model), where ADVI discrimination bias is less severe than in the multidimensional models (2+ dimensions) studied by Ulitzsch & Nestler; (ii) the contamination effect parameter γ enters linearly (additively in the logit) rather than as a discrimination-like multiplicative factor, so γ estimation is less sensitive to a_j bias—γ captures the mean shift attributable to exposure, which is orthogonal to item discriminability; (iii) we include a 2PL+γ fallback model (dropping c_j and d_j asymptote parameters) to be deployed if the full 4PL+ADVI shows discrimination instability under posterior predictive checks, reducing parameter count and ADVI approximation error; (iv) we run a targeted simulation: generate synthetic data from a known 4PL+γ model, introduce systematic a_j downward bias at the magnitudes reported by Ulitzsch & Nestler, re-fit the model via ADVI, and verify that γ and calibrated θ estimates remain within acceptable bounds (|bias| < 0.05 SD units for θ). This simulation takes <1 hour on CPU and directly quantifies the propagation of discrimination bias to our primary output quantities. Prior sensitivity analysis for γ: we use a weakly informative N(0, 1) prior and verify robustness by re-fitting with N(0, 0.5) and N(0, 2) priors—the ADVI fallback makes this feasible within our timeline since each VI fit takes minutes rather than hours. The calibrated score is computed by setting exposure = 0 and marginalizing: Acc_calibrated = E[P(correct | exposure=0)] with 95% credible intervals. This differs from PSN-IRT (which uses neural networks without contamination modeling or uncertainty), from DCR (which uses fuzzy inference without per-item tracking or uncertainty), from HiBayES (which provides Bayesian uncertainty but ignores contamination), from Growing Pains (which uses IRT with anchor items for efficient evaluation but does not model contamination), and from Silicon Bureaucracy (which audits contamination sensitivity but produces flags rather than calibrated scores). Exposure score noise propagation analysis: Self-Critique achieves AUC of only 0.60-0.70 at dataset level on RL-contaminated models—above random but far from perfect detection. Per-item exposure scores derived from Self-Critique carry substantial noise. We explicitly quantify how this noise propagates through IRT calibration via three approaches: (a) a calibrated noise injection study—we estimate Self-Critique's per-item false positive/negative rates from our controlled SFT experiments (where ground truth is known), inject Gaussian noise at matched levels (and at 1.5× and 2× those levels) into exposure scores, re-fit the IRT model, and measure the widening of posterior credible intervals for γ and calibrated θ; (b) we optionally extend the Bayesian model to treat exposure_ij as a noisy observation of latent true contamination τ_ij (exposure_ij ~ N(τ_ij, σ_e²)), allowing the model to propagate exposure uncertainty into posteriors automatically via Stan's measurement error modeling capabilities; (c) we report separate credible interval widths for SFT-trained models (where multiple detection signals are available and reliable) versus RL-trained models (where primarily Self-Critique is reliable), transparently showing that RL-model calibrated scores carry wider uncertainty. This analysis directly addresses the concern that the RL case study's conclusions depend on noisy inputs, and provides readers with quantitative guidance on how much to trust RL-model calibrated scores. Validation: We use controlled SFT contamination injection on small models (Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B/3B, Pythia-1B/1.4B, OLMo-1B) at 5 dosage levels (0%, 5%, 25%, 50%, 100% of benchmark items mixed as paraphrases into SFT data). Ground-truth uncontaminated accuracy is known from clean baselines, allowing us to verify that calibrated scores recover true performance within predicted credible intervals. We validate signal aggregation via leave-one-model-out cross-validation: for each held-out model, RRF weights are fixed (since RRF is training-free) and we verify that exposure scores on the held-out model predict actual contamination-driven score inflation. For the RL case, we frame this as an observational case study rather than full validation: we apply Self-Critique to existing instruction-tuned and RL-trained model families (Qwen2.5-Instruct, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-1.5B/7B) to estimate their RL contamination exposure, and demonstrate that the ContamCal framework produces plausible calibrated scores with uncertainty bounds. We explicitly acknowledge that without ground-truth contamination labels for these RL models, the calibrated scores represent our framework's best estimate rather than validated corrections—and we show via the noise propagation analysis that the wider credible intervals for RL models appropriately reflect this greater uncertainty. We validate the SFT-trained component against DCR's adjusted accuracy on the same benchmarks (MMLU, GSM8K, ARC-Challenge) for external consistency, and compare our controlled injection results with the characterizations from Yang et al. (arXiv 2601.06103) who use the same Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B models. 9. **[新增章节：Simulation-Based Identifiability Study]** — 在合成数据上系统性扫描 exposure AUC (0.90→0.55)、model count、prior 强度，报告 θ-γ recovery RMSE、posterior correlation、coverage、prior sensitivity、false positive robustness。建立 minimum AUC threshold + "low-confidence calibration" 标注机制。AUC=0.65 时显式报告 γ posterior width，若 95% CI > 15pp 则定义为"无法区分无污染与中度污染"。 — **命中 Soundness：直接回答"θ 和 γ 能否分离"这一核心技术质疑**

10. **[Validation]** — 重构为 dual-track design（原始版本仅有 controlled + 附属 RL case study）：Controlled track（内部有效性）= SFT injection with ground truth；Ecological track（外部有效性）= RL 模型 case study + 新 benchmark near-zero γ 验证（specificity）+ 已知 pretraining contamination 案例方向性验证。明确标注 ecological track 建立的是 directional external validity，full quantitative external validation 为 future work。 — **命中 Soundness + Significance：预防"controlled contamination 不反映真实场景"的质疑**

11. **[Validation / Scale]** — 新增 scale-bridging 实验：Qwen2.5-7B controlled SFT injection（原始版本上限 3B）。Ecological track 扩展至 Qwen2.5-72B observational analysis（仅推理，无需训练）。 — **命中 Soundness：将 controlled validation 规模从 3B 扩展至 7B，observational 覆盖至 72B**

12. **[Validation / RL 场景]** — RL 模型 claim 从"calibrated scores with wider CIs"重新定位为"contamination risk flagging with uncertainty quantification"。新增 expected regret 量化：在 top-5 model selection 具体场景中比较 uncalibrated vs. wide-CI calibrated vs. remove-and-rescore 的决策改善幅度。 — **命中 Significance：诚实定位比过度声称更可信，减少审稿人攻击面**

13. **[Validation / Ecological Validity]** — 新增 SFT vs. pretraining contamination 生态效度讨论，承认 SFT injection 为 "best-available proxy"，标注各信号对 pretraining contamination 的文献支撑程度（Min-K%++ 和 embedding retrieval 有文献支持，Self-Critique 和 ConStat 缺乏）。 — **命中 Soundness：坦诚讨论已知限制，比回避更不容易被攻击**

14. **[新增章节：Benchmarks]** — 明确列出 4 个验证 benchmark 及选择理由：MMLU（知识密集型多选）、ARC-Challenge（科学推理多选）、GSM8K（数学推理自由形式）、HumanEval（代码生成执行评估），覆盖三种主要 contamination 影响模式。Day-8 contingency 缩至 MMLU + GSM8K。 — **命中 Soundness：原始版本未指定 benchmark，容易被质疑 cherry-picking**

15. **[新增章节：Baseline Comparisons]** — 从 0 个 baseline 扩展至 5 个：Remove-and-rescore、Single-signal calibration (2PL+γ)、Vanilla IRT (4PL without γ)、Linear exposure regression + bootstrap CI（最简 post-hoc adjustment，测试是否需要 IRT 复杂度）、Emperor's New Clothes 中 2 种代表性 mitigation 策略（rephrasing = input transformation + option shuffling = evaluation transformation，明确选择理由避免 strawman 质疑）。 — **命中 Soundness：原始版本完全无 baseline，这是最大的 Occam's razor 风险**

16. **[新增章节：Evaluation Protocol]** — 定义 5 个量化 success metrics：calibration error (MAE)、interval coverage (50%/80%/95%)、interval sharpness、ranking preservation (Kendall's τ)、dose-response monotonicity。原始版本未定义任何评估指标。 — **命中 Soundness：低成本高收益，使"ContamCal 是否成功"可量化**

17. **[Signal Survival Analysis]** — 从"附属子分析"升格为 Supporting Contribution (5)，新增 signal × regime AUC heatmap 可视化建议，定位为 community reference。 — **命中 Novelty + Impact：增加论文贡献厚度和独立引用潜力**

18. **[新增章节：Limitations]** — 系统性列出 5 条已知限制（SFT proxy、RL 精度、规模上限、open-weight 要求、few-examinee prior 依赖），坦诚但有防御性论证。 — **命中 Soundness：主动暴露限制比被审稿人挖出更有利**

19. **[Resource Budget]** — 从 180 H100-hours 调整至 ~240 H100-hours（7B scale-bridging + 72B observational analysis 新增），新增 per-run cost order-of-magnitude 估计（Stage 1 signal computation + Stage 2 IRT fitting 分别估算）。 — **命中 Feasibility：使执行计划更具体可信**

【主要风险】:

1. **RL 模型校准精度的"so what"质疑**：即使经过 3 轮优化，RL 场景仍只能提供"contamination risk flagging with wide uncertainty bounds"而非精确校准。如果 3 位审稿人中有 2 位认为"对最重要的模型类别只能说'不确定'就等于没用"，significance 评分会被压到 borderline。Defense：expected regret 量化 + "诚实不确定性比虚假精度更有价值"的论证需在实验中用数字支撑，否则仅停留在rhetoric 层面。

2. **Novelty 的"组件组合"天花板**：核心方法是 4 个已有检测信号 + 已有 IRT 框架的组合。尽管有 circularity principle、item compromise 文献连接、paradigm complement 叙事等 framing 层面的加持，一位 novelty-focused 审稿人仍可能写"each component exists; the combination, while useful, is engineering rather than methodological novelty"。Defense：跨学科迁移的 3 个非平凡适配 + simulation-validated identifiability bounds 作为方法论贡献。

【实施评估】:
- **计算资源**: ~240 H100-hours 总预算。Stage 1 信号计算（4 个 detection signals × 多模型 × 4 benchmarks）按模型规模线性扩展；Stage 2 IRT fitting 依赖 benchmark item 数（GSM8K ~1.3K items 约分钟级，MMLU ~14K items 可能数小时，有 ADVI fallback 和 item subsampling 备选）。7B SFT 微调 ~20-40 H100-hours，72B 仅推理 ~10-20 H100-hours。需 multi-GPU 节点（建议 4-8×H100）。
- **数据需求**: 全部使用公开数据集和模型。Benchmarks: MMLU, ARC-Challenge, GSM8K, HumanEval（均公开）。Models: Qwen2.5 系列 (0.5B-72B), Pythia (1B/1.4B), OLMo (1B), DeepSeek-R1-Distill（均 open-weight）。Growing Pains 的 400+ model evaluation 数据用于 item parameter pre-estimation（需确认公开可用性）。Closed-weight model 的 2-signal mode case study 需 API 访问（ConStat + Self-Critique，成本可控）。
- **人工介入**: 最小。实验设计和代码实现为主要人力投入。SFT 污染注入为自动化流程（controlled 数据混入 + 微调）。Simulation study 完全自动化。不需要人工标注。论文撰写需人工（~2 周）。
- **训练需求**: 需要模型微调但**不**从头训练。SFT contamination injection 需对 7 个小模型 (0.5B-3B) + 1 个中模型 (7B) 做 5 个 dosage level 的 SFT 微调，共 ~40 次微调运行（大部分在小模型上，单次 <1 H100-hour）。72B 模型仅推理，不训练。IRT 模型用 Stan（NUTS/ADVI）拟合，非神经网络训练。
- **新 Benchmark**: 不需要构建新 benchmark。使用现有 4 个公开 benchmark。需构建 controlled contamination injection 的实验框架（将 benchmark 数据按比例混入 SFT 训练集），但这是实验工具而非新评测数据集。
- **预估周期**: 约 5-6 周从启动到可投稿。Week 1: 信号计算 pipeline 实现 + SFT injection 框架搭建；Week 2: 小模型 controlled experiments (0.5B-3B)；Week 3: 7B scale-bridging + simulation study + IRT fitting；Week 3-4: 72B observational + ecological track + baselines；Week 4-5: 分析 + 论文撰写；Week 5-6: revision + camera-ready 打磨。Day-8 contingency 可在 ~3 周内完成缩减版。ARR May 25 deadline 极紧张，建议保留 Day-8 contingency 作为硬性兜底。
