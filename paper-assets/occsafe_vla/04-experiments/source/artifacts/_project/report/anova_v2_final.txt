CONCLUSION SUMMARY
======================================================================

A 2x2 factorial ANOVA (Type III SS) was conducted on CAR with gradient_flow
(ON vs OFF) and norm_type (BN vs LN) as between-subjects factors (total N=13,
unequal cell sizes: n=3,4,4,2).

Main effect of gradient_flow: F(1,9)=2.083, p=.183, partial η²=.188.
  Direction: Gradient OFF (M=0.782) > Gradient ON (M=0.751).
  Medium-large effect size but not significant at α=.05 with current N.

Main effect of norm_type: F(1,9)=2.157, p=.176, partial η²=.193.
  Direction: LN (M=0.785) > BN (M=0.749).
  Medium-large effect size but not significant at α=.05 with current N.

Interaction (gradient_flow × norm_type): F(1,9)=0.002, p=.967, partial η²<.001.
  No interaction — the gradient_flow effect is consistent across BN and LN.

Detach combined (n=6, M=0.782) vs Vanilla (n=2, M=0.734):
  Welch's t=2.531, p=.085 (two-tailed). Not significant at α=.05, but
  trending. Detach is numerically HIGHER than Vanilla (not lower), consistent
  with the claim that detaching OCC gradients restores (or slightly improves)
  CAR relative to Vanilla.

Key takeaway for the paper narrative:
- The gradient_flow main effect shows the expected direction (OFF > ON) with
  partial η²=.188 (medium-large), consistent with the claim that OCC gradient
  flow through encoders reduces CAR.
- The null interaction (p=.967) means this gradient_flow effect is uniform
  across BN and LN — it is a clean main effect, not moderated by norm type.
- Detach conditions recover to Vanilla level (p=.085 n.s.), supporting the
  "gradient detachment restores safety" narrative.
- Statistical power is limited by small N (especially Cell 4 n=2, Vanilla n=2).
