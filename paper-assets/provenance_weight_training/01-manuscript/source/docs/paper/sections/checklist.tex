\section*{Responsible NLP Research Checklist}

\subsection*{A. For Every Submission}

\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item[\checkmark] \textbf{A1.} Did you describe the limitations of your work?
\textit{Yes, \S\ref{sec:limitations} discusses scale, contamination range, detector coverage, threshold, evaluation, and effect size limitations.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{A2.} Did you discuss any potential risks of your work?
\textit{Yes. The method assumes a pre-trained detector is available; deploying it with a poorly calibrated detector could degrade training quality (discussed in \S\ref{sec:limitations}, Detector coverage). The method does not generate new content or enable harmful uses beyond standard language model pretraining.}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{B. Did You Use or Create Scientific Artifacts?}

\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item[\checkmark] \textbf{B1.} Did you cite the creators of artifacts you used?
\textit{Yes. FineWeb~\citep{penedo2024fineweb}, Qwen3~\citep{yang2025qwen3}, and all baseline methods are cited in \S\ref{sec:experiments} and \S\ref{sec:related}.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{B2.} Did you discuss the license or terms for use and/or distribution of any artifacts?
\textit{FineWeb is released under Apache-2.0; Qwen3 under Apache-2.0. Our code and data will be released upon acceptance.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{B3.} Did you discuss if your use of existing artifacts was consistent with their intended use?
\textit{Yes. FineWeb is intended for language model training; we use it as a pretraining corpus. Qwen3 is used for both continued pretraining and synthetic data generation, consistent with its general-purpose design.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{B4.} Did you discuss the steps taken to check whether the data contains information that names or uniquely identifies individual people or offensive content?
\textit{FineWeb applies URL-level filtering and deduplication; we do not perform additional PII filtering as our focus is on contamination-aware weighting rather than content curation.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{B5.} Did you provide documentation of the artifacts?
\textit{Yes. \S\ref{sec:experiments} describes training data (132M tokens, FineWeb subset), synthetic data generation process, and detector specifications.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{B6.} Did you report relevant statistics like the number of examples, details of train/test/dev splits, etc.?
\textit{Yes. \S\ref{sec:experiments} reports token counts, contamination ratios, document statistics, and evaluation set size (5{,}000 documents, $\sim$4M tokens).}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{C. Did You Run Computational Experiments?}

\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item[\checkmark] \textbf{C1.} Did you report the number of parameters in the models used, the total computational budget, and computing infrastructure used?
\textit{Yes. Model sizes (0.6B, 1.7B) are reported in \S\ref{sec:experiments} and \S\ref{sec:robustness}. Training steps, batch sizes, and sequence lengths are in \S\ref{sec:experiments}. Infrastructure details: all experiments run on NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 (96\,GB) GPUs.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{C2.} Did you discuss the experimental setup, including hyperparameter search and best-found hyperparameter values?
\textit{Yes. \S\ref{sec:experiments} lists all hyperparameters (lr, optimizer, schedule). \S\ref{sec:sensitivity} reports sensitivity analysis for $\tau$ and $n_{\min}$. Grid search details in Appendix~\ref{app:grid}.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{C3.} Did you report descriptive statistics about your results?
\textit{Yes. Main results report 5-seed means $\pm$ standard deviation for the primary comparison (\S\ref{sec:main-results}); robustness analyses use 3 seeds. Paired $t$-tests with Bonferroni--Holm correction: $p_{\mathrm{BH}}{=}0.011$ at $\rho{=}0.4$ (5 seeds), $p{=}0.010$ at $\rho{=}0.6$ (3 seeds). Effect sizes reported alongside $p$-values.}

\item[\checkmark] \textbf{C4.} If you used existing packages, did you report the implementation, model, and parameter settings used?
\textit{Yes. We use HuggingFace Transformers for model loading and training, scikit-learn for TF-IDF+LR, and lm-evaluation-harness for downstream benchmarks. Settings are in \S\ref{sec:experiments}.}
\end{itemize}
