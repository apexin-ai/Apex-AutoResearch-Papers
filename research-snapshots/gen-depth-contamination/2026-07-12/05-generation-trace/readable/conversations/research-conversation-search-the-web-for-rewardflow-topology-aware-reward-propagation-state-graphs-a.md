### Paper 2: InfoTree (arXiv 2605.05262, May 2026)

**Title:** Maximizing Rollout Informativeness under a Fixed Budget: A Submodular View of Tree Search for Tool-Use Agentic Reinforcement Learning

**What it proposes:**
InfoTree formalizes Rollout Informativeness under a Fixed Budget (RIFB) as the expected policy-gradient mass that a tool-use rollout set injects into Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). It recasts tree construction as a monotone submodular maximization problem, with the greedy solution being the UUCB selector.

**Key technical approach:**
- Formalizes rollout informativeness as a scalar objective under budget constraints
- Proves that independent sampling suffers persistent collapse under sparse rewards
- Casts tree construction as monotone submodular maximization
- Uses greedy optimization with UUCB (Upper Confidence Bound) selector

**Key results:**
- Outperforms flat GRPO, DeepSearch, Tree-GRPO, AT2PO, CW-GRPO, and RC-GRPO
- Evaluated across nine diverse benchmarks:

  - Web-search agents: GAIA, HLE-100, BrowseComp-lite
