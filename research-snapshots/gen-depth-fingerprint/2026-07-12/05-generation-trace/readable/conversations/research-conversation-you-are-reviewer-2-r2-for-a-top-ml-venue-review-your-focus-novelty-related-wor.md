## Paper Summary
Title: "Generation Depth Fingerprinting via Differential Depth Response in Non-Paraphrastic LLM Chains"

The paper formulates "generation depth classification" (gen-0 / gen-1 / gen-2+) as a new task beyond binary AI-text detection. It introduces Differential Depth Response (DDR): when text is recursively generated through non-paraphrastic chains, central-tendency features (entropy mean, TTR, hapax ratio) of NLL distributions drift monotonically with depth, while shape features (entropy max, tail decay) remain near-stationary.

**Contributions claimed**:
1. Task formulation: generation depth classification (gen-0/gen-1/gen-2+) in non-paraphrastic continuation chains
2. Empirical characterization of DDR across 8 generators, 5 families, 3 prompt types, 4 domains + causality analysis
3. Negative finding: depth-aware data curation ≈ random subsampling for downstream CLM quality

**Related Work (Section 2)**:
- Perplexity-based detection: DetectGPT, FastDetectGPT, Binoculars, GLTR, DNA-GPT — all binary classifiers
- Distributional signatures/LD-Score: character-level detection, used as baseline and augmentation feature
- Model collapse: Shumailov et al. (2024), Dohmatob et al. (2024) — aggregate distributional consequences, not individual text depth
- Iterative generation chains: Briesch et al. (2025) "LLM as Broken Telephone" — paraphrastic chains with attractor convergence
- Vocabulary diversity/stylometry: TTR, hapax — repurposed as depth-discriminative features
- Data quality/curation: Wenzek et al. (2019) CCNet

**Method**: 7 handcrafted features from NLL distribution under Pythia-410M reference model → XGBoost + LOGO evaluation. Binary F1 = 0.694 (LOGO), 0.830 (cross-model chains).

**Key experimental results**:
- LOGO 5-gen average: macro F1 = 0.804, binary F1 = 0.694
- Baseline comparison: 7-feat (0.798) > Avg-LogProb (0.765) > LD-Score (0.721) > FastDetectGPT (0.627) > Binoculars (0.579)
- Cross-model chains: binary F1 = 0.830
- Cross-reference validation: 3 models all preserve DDR
- DDR causality: 6/7 Granger-significant, 5.3× permutation gap
- Downstream: negative finding (TOST equivalence)
- Instruct models: substantial degradation (base-only scope)
- 5-class ceiling at ~0.50
- QLoRA end-to-end: +10–15 pp over 7-feat but 15× cost
- Temperature: degrades at t > 0.9
- Length confound controlled
- Paraphrastic chains: much weaker signal (0.62 vs 0.781)

**Paper structure**: 8 pages main text + 6 pages appendix (A–L). 11 references.

4	The central finding is that recursive generation alters \emph{where} the token-level surprise distribution sits but not \emph{how} it is shaped: autoregressive sampling with fixed top-$p$ plausibly truncates the conditional distribution at a consistent shape regardless of input, while mean surprisal systematically decreases as the model conditions on its own increasingly typical outputs.
