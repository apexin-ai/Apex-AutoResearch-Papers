**Strengths**: Thorough ablation, multi-model coverage, honest reporting, strong baseline battery (especially regex B5 at 99.33%), above-average statistical practice.

**Weaknesses**:
- W1: Core finding near-trivial. Regex B5 achieves 99.33%. SFT adds only 0.50pp (6 samples). The "semantic understanding" claim rests on 8 boundary samples.
- W2: Dataset too easy and narrow. HumanEval/MBPP short algorithmic puzzles where fails almost always raise exceptions. Real-world faults include silent logic errors.
- W3: Cross-language OOD results may be imagined/fabricated (not found in actual paper).
- W4: Most conditions use single seed. The tier hierarchy rests on single-seed data.
- W5: VOTA cross-model gap confounded by training-time truncation (DeepSeek 91.7% truncated).
- W6: Budget curve "phase transition" is a natural consequence of exception position in trace, not a discovery.
- W7: DebugBench has severe ceiling effect (no_trace already 96.71%).
- W8: Solutions generated by evaluation model (Qwen), potential familiarity bias.

**Missing experiments**: Silent logic error dataset, cross-condition multi-seed, marker randomization, simple classifier baseline (logistic regression on TF-IDF).
