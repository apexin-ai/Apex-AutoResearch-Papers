## Overview

EDGE-GRPO is an algorithm that adopts Entropy-Driven Advantage and Guided Error Correction to effectively mitigate the problem of advantage collapse.

## Problem It Addresses

The Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) algorithm, which relies on sparse reward rules, often encounters the issue of identical rewards within groups, leading to the advantage collapse problem.

## Key Components

The algorithm has two main components:

1. **Guided Error Correction (GEC)**: At the response level, GEC is introduced to enhance response diversity, providing more effective guidance even when the model encounters questions beyond its current capacity.

2. **Entropy-Driven Advantage (EDA)**: At the signal level, EDA assigns higher advantages to correct responses with low entropy and lower advantages to incorrect responses with low entropy, thereby increasing the diversity of the advantage signal.

## Performance

The method achieves substantial performance gains compared to the vanilla GRPO across multiple reasoning benchmarks, reaching comparable performance to other open-source models using only 1K training samples.

**Advantage Normalization in GRPO:** Group-Relative Advantage Normalization is a class of techniques for variance normalization and adaptive scaling in policy gradient reinforcement learning, serving as a variance reduction and stability mechanism by centering and scaling advantage or reward signals within a per-prompt, per-seed, per-agent, or per-sample group.
