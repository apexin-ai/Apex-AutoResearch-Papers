## Paper Summary

**Title:** Phase-Dependent Compound Sparsity: Strategy-Specific Severity Emerges from the Interaction of Head Pruning and KV Cache Eviction

**Core claim:** Compound sparsity (joint head pruning + KV cache eviction) produces phase-dependent interaction severity that varies by eviction strategy and reasoning phase. The paper is positioned as an empirical finding paper, not a method contribution.

**Five stated contributions:**
1. Discovery of phase-dependent compound sparsity severity as emergent property, replicated across 3 benchmarks and 3 architectures
2. Quantified dose-response relationship with sub-exponential growth
3. Three-way dissociation (importance sig, random weak/null, recency null) as novel experimental design for ruling out confounds
4. Discrimination collapse as primary mechanism (Gini/CV decrease under head pruning)
5. Cross-benchmark analysis: strategy specificity correlates with reasoning chain structure, not task difficulty

**Related Work (Section 2):**
- Compound Compression: SparK [1] (pruning+quantization, uniform super-additive), SLiM [2] (joint pruning-eviction, no phase analysis)
- Phase-Aware KV Cache: ThinKV [3] (phase-aware single-axis), R-KV [4] (reasoning-optimized, single-axis)
- Compression Phase Transitions: [5] (physics of KV cache compression, orthogonal "phase" meaning)
- Importance Score Collapse: HIES [6] (entropy-based compensation, single-axis)
- Adaptive Eviction: Lethe [7], H2O [8], StreamingLLM [9]

Total references: 9

**Method details:**
- Interaction Index: II_i = PPL(both) - PPL(prune) - PPL(evict) + PPL(base)
- Phase segmentation via tercile splits of uncompressed per-token PPL
- Three eviction strategies: importance (H2O), random, recency
- Paired Wilcoxon with Bonferroni, Cohen's dz, bootstrap CIs
- Models: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B (all 7-8B)
- Benchmarks: MATH-500, GPQA Diamond, GSM8K

**Key results:**
- Importance gradient on GPQA: 0.947 (s=0.15) to 10.68 (s=0.30), dz=0.72–1.22
- Three-way dissociation at s=0.25: importance 8.62 vs random 0.76 vs recency ≈0
- Cross-benchmark specificity: MATH-500 (15–17×) > GPQA (7.8–11.3×) > GSM8K (2.3–3.8×)
- LLaMA ratio only 2.0–2.6× (architecture-dependent)
- Pilot phase-aware allocation: 18% PPL recovery but CIs cross zero

**Limitations acknowledged:** 7B models only, GSM8K ratios modest, recency n=100, no wall-clock throughput, empirical understanding not deployable method.
